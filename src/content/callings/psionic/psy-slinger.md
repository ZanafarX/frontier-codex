You channel the Current through your firearm, loading bullets with
memory and will. Your shots bend around corners, punch through walls,
and strike true when physics says they shouldn't.

## Starting Choices

- **Prime Attribute:** **+1 QUICK** (fixed, no choice).
- **Skill Allocation:** Distribute **6 Skill Points** among Career
  Skills (maximum starting Rank 2).
- **Starting Talents:** You start with **Ghost Bullet (Rank 1
  Cantrip)** automatically. Choose **one general Mundane Talent**
  (Combat or Non-Combat list — player's choice).
- **Starting Erosion:** 1 (the cost of the gift).
- **Note on starting gear:** The Rune-Etched Revolver is a unique
  Calling-specific item that mechanically matches a Peacemaker (WR 3,
  Tempo 4) but additionally serves as your Psionic Focus.
