You are an expert in the machines of the new world. You might be a
train engineer, a safecracker, or a demolitionist. While the
Muck-Raker builds, you dismantle. While the High-Roller talks their
way in, you crawl through the vents.

## Starting Choices

- **Prime Attribute:** Choose **+1 QUICK** or **+1 SAVVY**.
- **Skill Allocation:** Distribute **6 Skill Points** among Career
  Skills (maximum starting Rank 2).
- **Specialist Gear:** Choose **Satchel Charge** (Volatile explosive)
  *or* **Clockwork Canary** (detects gas / spirits). Both are listed in
  starting_gear for reference; you take only one.
- **Starting Talents:** Choose **one Rail-Jack Talent** from the
  Calling-specific list above, plus **one general Mundane Talent**
  (Combat or Non-Combat list — player's choice).
