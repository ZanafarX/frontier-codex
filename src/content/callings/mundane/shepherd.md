You tend to the wounded, the sick, and the dying. You might be a
frontier doctor, a Redeemer priest, or simply someone who learned that
healing is the most valuable skill in a world that bleeds.

## Starting Choices

- **Prime Attribute:** Choose **+1 SAVVY** or **+1 ECHO**.
- **Skill Allocation:** Distribute **6 Skill Points** among Career
  Skills (maximum starting Rank 2).
- **Secondary Gear:** Choose **Coach Gun** (WR 4, Tempo 4, Scatter) *or*
  **Scripture Book** (+1 Roll Bonus to Persuasion with the faithful).
  Both are listed in starting_gear for reference; you take only one.
- **Starting Talents:** Choose **one Shepherd Talent** from the
  Calling-specific list above, plus **one general Mundane Talent**
  (Combat or Non-Combat list — player's choice).
