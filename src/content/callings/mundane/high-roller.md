You survive on charm, luck, and the ability to read a room. You might
be a gambler, a con artist, a salesman, or simply someone who's
learned that words are cheaper than bullets.

## Starting Choices

- **Prime Attribute:** Choose **+1 SWAY** or **+1 SAVVY**.
- **Skill Allocation:** Distribute **6 Skill Points** among Career
  Skills (maximum starting Rank 2).
- **Starting Cheat Tool:** Choose **Marked Cards** *or* **Loaded Dice**.
  Both are listed in starting_gear for reference; you take only one.
- **Starting Talents:** Choose **one High-Roller Talent** from the
  Calling-specific list above, plus **one general Mundane Talent**
  (Combat or Non-Combat list, player's choice).
