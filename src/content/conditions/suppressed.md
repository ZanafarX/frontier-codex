---
name: "Suppressed"
short_effect: "−2 dice (Pool Penalty) to all actions. You are rattled, flinching, unable to focus."
duration: "Rest of scene, or until cleared"
inflicted_by:
  - "Near-miss from gunfire (attack misses by Margin 1-2)"
  - "Failing a Fear check"
  - "Fen-Wraith Fear Aura, Stormcaller Static Burst, other Veil-Born terror effects"
  - "Iron Revenant Boiler Scream"
  - "Redeemer Inquisitor's The Sermon"
  - "Frightened condition resolving (after 1 round of fleeing)"
  - "Psionic effects (Fearmonger, The Fatal Reading, Mass Hysteria, The Unmask success, Prophecy of Doom)"
cleared_by:
  - "Spend 1 Mettle (shake it off)"
  - "Recover action (Tempo 6)"
  - "Iron Calm (Bastion Soul R1, if from supernatural source)"
  - "Adrenaline Purge (Flesh Shaper R2)"
  - "Burden Transfer (Bastion Soul R2, absorbed by another)"
severity: "moderate"
---

Suppression is the workhorse status of *The Veil & Lead*: the default
"you got rattled" outcome of near-misses, fear, and shock. The −2 dice
penalty (Pool Penalty) is significant but not crippling, and it can be
shaken off with a Mettle expenditure or six Ticks of Recover.

The condition is mechanically identical whether the cause is mundane
(a bullet that snapped past your ear) or supernatural (a Fen-Wraith's
silent scream pressing against your spine). The fiction differs; the
math doesn't.
