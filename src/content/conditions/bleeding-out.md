name: "Bleeding Out"
short_effect: "You are Downed at 0 Vitality. Lose 1 Vitality at the start of each of your turns. Die at −(IRON + 2) Vitality."
duration: "Until Stabilized, killed, or you reach the Death Threshold"
inflicted_by:
  - "Vitality reduced to 0 (Downed)"
  - "Grievous Wound roll of 8 (Arterial Hit) — bleeds out even with HP remaining"
cleared_by:
  - "Stabilization: Medicine check (Tempo 6, TN 11 + how far below 0 the patient is)"
  - "Medical Kit (+2 Roll Bonus to Stabilization)"
  - "Anchor the Dying (Bastion Soul R1) — stops the bleeding for the rest of the scene"
  - "Blood Rouse (Flesh Shaper R2) — stabilizes AND restores 2 HP"
  - "Knit Flesh / other psionic healing brings HP above 0"
severity: "severe"

When you reach 0 Vitality, you are **Downed.** You collapse where you
stand. You cannot take Actions except **Crawl (2m, Tempo 6)** or
**Speak (Tempo 0).** At the start of each of your turns, you lose 1
Vitality. You die when your Vitality reaches **−(IRON + 2).**

| IRON | Death At |
| :---: | :---: |
| 1 | −3 Vitality |
| 2 | −4 Vitality |
| 3 | −5 Vitality |
| 4 | −6 Vitality |
| 5 | −7 Vitality |

Higher IRON gives you more time — but not much. A Downed character is
**not out of the fight** — they can call warnings, share information,
even crawl toward cover — but they are dying.

| Patient Vitality | Stabilization TN |
| :---: | :---: |
| 0 | 11 |
| −1 | 12 |
| −2 | 13 |
| −3 | 14 |

**Arterial Hit edge case:** A Grievous Wound roll of 8 inflicts
Bleeding Out *even if HP remains above 0.* The character is conscious
and standing, but hemorrhaging. Stabilize before the next turn or
start losing Vitality.
