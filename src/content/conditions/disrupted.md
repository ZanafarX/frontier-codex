name: "Disrupted"
short_effect: "Your Channeling fails immediately. The Talent's Cost is spent (Strain/Erosion/Memory). You take 1 additional Strain from feedback."
duration: "Resolves immediately (the Talent fails on the spot)"
inflicted_by:
  - "Taking any damage while in the Open State, then failing the Focus check (ECHO + Focus vs. TN 8 + damage taken)"
  - "Being hit by a Salt Round while in the Open State (automatic — no Focus check allowed)"
  - "Being doused in rock salt while in the Open State (automatic — no Focus check allowed)"
  - "Disrupt (Bastion Soul R1) — Reaction-triggered psychic interference (no damage required)"
  - "Other Salt-based or anti-Psionic effects targeting an Open Psionic"
cleared_by:
  - "Resolves immediately — there is no 'cleared' state; the Talent simply fails"
  - "Cost is not refunded (Strain/Erosion/Memory already paid is gone)"
severity: "moderate"
---

Disrupted is the consequence of breaking concentration during the Open
State. Unlike most conditions, it doesn't *linger*; it resolves the
moment it occurs by canceling the in-progress Channel. The Talent
fails, the Cost is spent regardless, and the caster takes **+1 Strain
of feedback** on top of whatever caused the disruption in the first
place.

The Focus check formula is **ECHO + Focus vs. TN 8 + damage taken.**
Wounded (−1 die) and other penalties stack. The **Veil Anchor** item
provides +2 Roll Bonus to this check. A determined Psionic with high
Focus can shrug off small hits and keep channeling, but any Salt-based
disruption (Salt Rounds, Salt Pouches, rock salt) bypasses the check
entirely.

Disrupt (the Bastion Soul Cantrip) is the rare exception that causes
Disruption *without* physical damage: a Reaction that hits Close-range
casters with a psychic interference pulse, forcing the same Focus
check at TN 11.

(See the **Open State** condition and keyword for the underlying
vulnerability, and the **Backlash** keyword for the related but
distinct Bleed-Die-1s mechanic.)
