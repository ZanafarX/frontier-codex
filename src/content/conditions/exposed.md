---
name: "Exposed"
short_effect: "Attackers gain TN −1 against you (easier to hit)."
duration: "Until start of your next turn (Tempo-triggered), or until you reach cover (positional)"
inflicted_by:
  - "Completing an action with Tempo 3 or less (most Fast Actions)"
  - "Being caught in the open without cover"
  - "Standing up from Prone (no longer Prone, but visibly upright)"
cleared_by:
  - "Start of your next turn (automatic, for Tempo-triggered Exposure)"
  - "Reaching cover (for positional Exposure)"
  - "Take Cover action (Tempo 3)"
severity: "minor"
---

Exposed is the tax on speed. Most Fast Actions (Tempo 2-3) trigger
Exposed (drawing your weapon, scrambling between cover, aiming) because
moving fast means your eyes and body aren't where they need to be to
defend yourself.

The penalty is one-sided (it affects attackers' TN, not your own pool),
which makes it a less devastating debuff than Suppressed but a
consistent one. A gunslinger who keeps drawing fresh weapons and
scrambling between positions is *always* Exposed somewhere between
turns. The tactical question is whether speed is worth the −1 TN cost.
