Wounded is a Vitality threshold, not a combat status — it begins the
moment your HP drops to or below half your maximum and persists until
healing lifts you back above the line. The −1 die penalty applies to
**all physical actions** (Pool Penalty), and the source notes that
crossing further thresholds (with optional rules) imposes additional
−1 die penalties for severely-injured characters.

Wounded is the most common debuff in extended combat. A character with
13 maximum Vitality is Wounded at 6 HP and below — which, with a few
solid hits taken, can happen quickly. Healers (Flesh Shapers,
Shepherds) earn their place by keeping the party above this line.

The Health States summary for reference:

| State | Threshold | Effect |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Healthy | Above half Vitality | None |
| Wounded | At or below half Vitality | −1 die physical |
| Downed | 0 Vitality | Bleeding Out |
| Dead | Negative Vitality ≥ (IRON + 2) | Gone |
