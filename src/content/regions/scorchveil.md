A vast, merciless expanse that kills unprepared travelers within days.
Temperatures swing from 120°F at noon to below freezing at midnight.
The sand seems semi-sentient, shifting to cover tracks or expose
ruins.

This is the domain of the **Dust Vultures**, who have mastered
**Sand-Sailing** using wind-skiffs. Ironbrand contracts occasionally
bring mercenary squads into the desert, but they are visitors. The
Vultures are the land, and the land is the Vultures.

## Threats & Features

| Feature | Effect |
| :------ | :----- |
| Ashborn | Dust-spirits stirred up by storms. HP 2, Defense 11. Reform unless killed by water or Psionic attack. |
| Mirages | Current-illusions. Interacting causes 1 Strain. Insight (TN 13) to identify before engagement. |
| Quicksand | QUICK + Athletics (TN 11) or sink. Allies can pull a sinking comrade free with IRON (TN 13). |

## Settlements

**Greystone** is the cliff-fortress town built into a mesa face on the
Scorchveil's edge, accessible only by steam-winch lift. Source also
names **Brannoch**, a fortified trail outpost serving as neutral
smuggler ground ("Ask No Questions, Tell No Lies"); no settlement
file yet.

## Landmarks

**Ojo del Diablo**, the twenty-mile sinkhole lake at the desert's
heart, is the only currently-filed landmark. It is a Stable Breach
where time runs sideways.
