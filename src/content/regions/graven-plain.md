From a distance, the Graven Plain looks idyllic: an ocean of yellow
grass. Up close, you realize the hills are burial mounds. The ground
is paved with bones: rib cages the size of houses, femurs thick as
oaks. **Whatever died here was colossal.**

No single faction holds the Plain. The **Circle of Ash** maintains
their hidden valley sanctuary at Palomera. The **Redeemers** run
patrols through the open grassland, burning anything that crawls out
of the mounds. The Plain itself does most of the killing.

## Threats & Features

| Feature | Effect |
| :------ | :----- |
| Marrow Downs | Central hills. Digging releases "Grave Gas": a hallucinogen that makes the user believe they are an ancient giant. GRIT (TN 13) or 1d6 hours of delusion. |
| Stone Circles | Pre-human ritual sites. Psionic Talents cast inside are **+1 Rank effect**, but **Backlash is doubled**. |
| Disappearances | Children who roll down hills sometimes vanish; locals report hearing them giggling underground for days. |

## Settlements

**Palomera** is the hidden Circle sanctuary, cloaked in psionic
illusions in a valley that looks like a deadly rockslide from
outside. It serves the Hollowborn fleeing Redeemer persecution.

## Landmarks

No landmarks from the planned set are in this region. Source names
two: **Uphill River** (a stream that flows uphill against gravity, the
Drowned wading slowly upstream against the current) and
**Bell-in-the-Black** (a 50-foot pillar of black obsidian that tolls
like a church bell during storms, the sound speaking Suggestions into
hearers' minds). Neither has a landmark file yet.
