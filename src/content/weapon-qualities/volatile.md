---
name: "Volatile"
short_effect: "On Fumble, weapon misfires: user takes 1d3 damage and weapon jams."
applies_to: ["all"]
tags: ["risk"]
---

Volatile weapons are dangerous to their wielder. The Gatling Gun's
overheating mechanism, satchel charges with unstable fuses, and any weapon
loaded with Ghost-Rock ammunition. The misfire damage is in addition to
the action being wasted.
