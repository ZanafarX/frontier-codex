---
name: "Reliable"
short_effect: "This weapon never jams on Fumbles; only on specific malfunctions."
applies_to: ["firearm"]
tags: ["dependability"]
---

Reliable weapons have proven mechanisms that resist the worst of bad luck.
A Reliable weapon will only jam through specific in-fiction malfunctions
(damaged in a fall, exposed to fouling), never simply because the dice
came up cold.
