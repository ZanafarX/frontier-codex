---
name: "Area Attack"
short_effect: "Hits all targets in a defined area with a single attack roll."
applies_to: ["firearm"]
tags: ["area"]
---

Area Attack weapons saturate a region rather than targeting individuals.
The Gatling Gun's 5m cone is the standard example. The single attack roll
is compared to each target's Defense individually; some may be hit and
others may not.
