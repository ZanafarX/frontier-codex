---
name: "Rapid Cycle"
short_effect: "Can fire twice without moving (second shot at +2 Tempo, no Aim bonus)."
applies_to: ["firearm"]
tags: ["rate-of-fire"]
---

Rapid Cycle weapons allow follow-up shots through lever actions, internal
magazines, or other mechanisms that minimize the time between rounds. The
follow-up shot is faster than reacquiring the target normally, but the
shooter has no time to aim properly.
