name: "Reach"
short_effect: "Can attack targets up to 3m away; +1 TN to attack targets at Point-Blank."
applies_to: ["melee"]
tags: ["range"]
---

Reach weapons trade close-quarters utility for extended striking distance.
Spears, lances, and polearms can keep enemies at bay but become awkward
when an opponent closes inside the weapon's optimal range.
