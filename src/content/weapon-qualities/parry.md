---
name: "Parry"
short_effect: "+1 Defense when wielded — you can use the weapon to deflect."
applies_to: ["melee"]
tags: ["defensive"]
---

Parry weapons are designed for active defense as much as offense: sabers,
fencing blades, dueling sticks. The Defense bonus applies whenever the
weapon is in hand and the wielder is not Surprised, Stunned, or otherwise
prevented from defending.
