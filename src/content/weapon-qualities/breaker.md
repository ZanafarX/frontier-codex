---
name: "Breaker"
short_effect: "Armor Piercing 1 — Ignore 1 point of Armor Rating."
applies_to: ["all"]
tags: ["armor-piercing"]
---

Breaker weapons concentrate force into a small area on impact, punching
through armor that would deflect a less focused attack. Reduces the
target's effective Armor Rating by 1 for damage calculation. Cannot
reduce AR below 0.
