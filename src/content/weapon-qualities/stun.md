---
name: "Stun"
short_effect: "On a Critical hit, the target must roll GRIT + Resilience vs TN 11 or become Stunned."
applies_to: ["all"]
tags: ["status"]
---

Stun weapons deliver concussive force — heavy impact, blunt edges, weighted
strikes. Brass Knuckles, the Sledgehammer, and similar weapons can rattle
a target's senses on a clean hit. The Stunned condition prevents most
actions for one turn.
