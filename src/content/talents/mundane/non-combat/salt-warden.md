## Effect

**Resource cost:** 1 Salt Pouch use (consumed).

When you lay a salt circle during camp, it provides enhanced protection
beyond the normal effect (preventing spirit intrusion):

- The Tension Pool is reduced by **1 die** at the start of rest.
- All **ECHO + Resilience** checks against Fear made within the circle
  gain **+1 Roll Bonus** until dawn.
- Without a salt circle, Strain does not recover during rest. Your
  enhanced circle additionally allows **1 point of Erosion recovery**
  during a Long Rest inside it (normally Erosion does not recover from
  rest at all; this is the only mundane method).
