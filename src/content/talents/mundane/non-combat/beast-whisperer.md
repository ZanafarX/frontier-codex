## Effect

Spend 10 minutes with a domesticated or wild animal. Make a
**SWAY + Handling** check:

- **TN 11 (domesticated):** Learn whether other humans have passed
  through recently, the direction of water, or the general "mood" of
  the area.
- **TN 13 (wild):** As above, plus whether predators are nearby or
  Veil-Born creatures have been active.
- **TN 15 (predator):** As above, plus the predator's hunting territory
  and recent kills.

## Special

Animals you've spent a full day bonding with no longer bolt during Veil
events (supernatural fear). They trust you enough to hold steady,
though they remain visibly distressed.
