## Effect

**Resource cost:** 1 ration + 1 hour of time.

When you share a meal and genuine conversation with an NPC or group
during Downtime, their Disposition toward your party improves by one
step: **Hostile → Unfriendly → Neutral → Friendly → Allied**. The shift
lasts until you or your allies do something to worsen it.

## Limit

Cannot affect the same NPC more than once per session. The NPC must
share the meal willingly — this is not coercion.
