## Effect

Each day of travel through non-desert terrain, you may gather enough
materials for **one** of the following:

- **Poison Antidote:** Reduces the TN of Poison resistance checks by 2.
- **Fever Reducer:** Target recovers 1 additional Strain during their
  next Short Rest.
- **Wound Salve:** The next Medicine check on the target heals +1
  additional HP.

## Limit

You can carry a maximum of **3 remedies** at a time (they spoil).
Cannot gather in desert terrain (Scorchveil) or the Ash Belt. There's
nothing growing.
