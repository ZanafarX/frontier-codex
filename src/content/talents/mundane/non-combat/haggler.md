## Effect

When buying or trading, you pay **one Cost Rating less** than listed
(minimum Cost Rating 1). When selling, you receive **one Cost Rating
more** than the item is normally worth. Additionally, you can assess
the true value of any item by examining it for 1 minute — the GM tells
you its actual Cost Rating and whether it has hidden properties
(modified, counterfeit, cursed).
