## Effect

You can create detailed maps of areas you've explored (requires
**1 hour per significant area**). These maps have mechanical weight:
any character following your map gains **+2 Roll Bonus** to Navigation
checks in that area and cannot become lost. Completed maps of unmapped
dangerous regions are valuable trade goods (Cost Rating 2–3 depending
on the area's danger).

## Special

Your maps are immune to the "shifting terrain" effect that some
Veil-touched areas exhibit. The act of documenting reality helps
anchor it.
