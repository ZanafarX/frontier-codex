## Effect

You can read Pre-Rupture inscriptions, understand fragments of
Veil-Born speech, and identify the purpose of Pre-Rupture artifacts
without an Engineering check. Once per session, you may ask the GM one
question about a Veil phenomenon, ruin, or artifact and receive a
truthful (if cryptic) answer.

## Special

Speaking the Old Tongue aloud adds **+1 die to the Tension Pool**. The
Veil listens when its language is spoken.
