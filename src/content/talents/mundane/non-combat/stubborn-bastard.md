## Effect

Once per session, when you fail any **non-combat check**, you may
immediately retry the same check at the same TN with no additional
penalty. The second result stands regardless.

## Special

If the retry also fails, you take **1 Strain** from frustration and
physical exertion.

## Limit

Once per session. Cannot be used on combat rolls (attack, defense,
initiative).
