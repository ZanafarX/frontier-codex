## Effect

When wielding a weapon with **Tempo 3 or less** (Bowie Knife, Brass
Knuckles), you may use **QUICK instead of IRON** for melee attack rolls.
Additionally, your standard Strikes with these weapons no longer trigger
the **Exposed** condition.
