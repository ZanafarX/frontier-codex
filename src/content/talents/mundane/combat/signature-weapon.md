## Effect

You bond with a specific weapon — firearm, melee weapon, or thrown
weapon. While wielding your Signature Weapon:

- You gain **+1 Roll Bonus** to all attack rolls made with it (added to
  your sum after rolling).
- You can never be permanently **Disarmed** of it — if dropped, knocked
  away, or taken, you instinctively know its exact location within Far
  range (50m).
- If your Signature Weapon is destroyed, you suffer **1 Strain** (the
  loss is psychologically jarring). You may bond with a new weapon after
  1 full week.

## Limit

You may only have one Signature Weapon at a time.
