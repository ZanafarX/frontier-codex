## Effect

Issue a Command. Choose up to **3 allies** who can hear you. Each gains
one of the following (your choice, individually assigned):

- **Advance:** Move up to 3 meters immediately (no Exposed trigger).
- **Focus:** +1 Roll Bonus to their next attack this scene.
- **Rally:** Clear the **Suppressed** condition immediately.

## Limit

Once per scene.
