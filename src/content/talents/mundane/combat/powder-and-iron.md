## Effect

When dual-wielding pistols, you may attack two different targets as a
single action. Make one attack roll with a **−1 Roll Penalty**. Apply
the result against both targets' Defense separately. Each hit deals its
respective weapon's normal damage (WR + Steps − target's AR, calculated
individually).
