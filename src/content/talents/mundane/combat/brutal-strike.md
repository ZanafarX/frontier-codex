## Effect

Before making a melee attack, spend 1 Mettle. If the attack hits, add
**+3 damage** and the target must Resist or be **Staggered** (−2 dice
to next action).

## Resist

GRIT + Resilience vs. TN 11. On success, the target is not Staggered;
damage still applies.
