## Effect

When you attack from a hidden position (successful Stealth check, per
standard rules), your first attack gains **+2 Roll Bonus** and the
target cannot use Reactions (Dive, Dodge, Parry, Intercept) against it.
Additionally, you gain **+2 Roll Bonus** to Initiative rolls in
encounters where you had time to prepare the ambush position.
