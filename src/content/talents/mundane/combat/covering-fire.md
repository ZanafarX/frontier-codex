## Effect

As an action (Tempo 5), designate an area (up to 10m radius) within your
weapon's range. Until the start of your next turn, any enemy who moves
or attacks from within that area provokes an immediate attack from you
(one attack per enemy, uses ammunition normally).

## Special

Requires ammunition for each provoked attack.
