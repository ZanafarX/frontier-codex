## Effect

When you have a target Grappled, you gain **+2 Roll Bonus** to all
opposed Brawl checks to maintain the grapple. Additionally, at the start
of each of your turns while grappling, the target takes **1 damage**
(crushing pressure, ignores AR). You may choose to deal this as
**Strain** instead of HP damage.
