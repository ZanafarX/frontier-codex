## Effect

When you hit with an unarmed or improvised weapon attack, choose one
effect:

- **Blind:** Target gains the Blinded condition (−4 dice) until the end
  of their next action.
- **Stagger:** Target's next action has +2 Tempo (they act later on The
  Count).
- **Disarm:** Target drops one held item. It lands at their feet
  (requires Interact, Tempo 4, to retrieve).

## Resist

GRIT + Resilience vs. TN 11. On success, the rider effect is negated
(damage still applies).
