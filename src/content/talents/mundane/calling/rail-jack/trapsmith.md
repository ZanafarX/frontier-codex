## Effect

**Resource cost:** 1 Mettle + materials.

Given 10 minutes and basic materials (wire, nails, gunpowder, or a
single explosive), you can construct one of the following traps in an
area you control:

- **Tripwire Alarm:** Alerts you when someone enters the area. No
  damage, but grants you automatic Initiative (Tick 2) against the
  intruder.
- **Nail Bomb:** Deals **WR 3 (Scatter)** to all creatures within
  Close range when triggered.
- **Snare:** Target is **Grappled**. Deals no damage.

## Resist

- Nail Bomb: **QUICK + Athletics vs. TN 13** to avoid.
- Snare: **IRON + Athletics vs. TN 13** to escape.

## Special

You can disarm any mundane trap you encounter automatically (no check),
and you identify the trap type and trigger mechanism on sight.
