## Effect

You can squeeze through any opening large enough to fit your head.
Movement through tight spaces (vents, crawlspaces, between bars) does
**not reduce your Speed**. Additionally, you gain **+2 Roll Bonus** to
all checks to escape Grapples, bindings, or restraints.

## Special

You automatically identify alternate routes through buildings — vents,
maintenance tunnels, crawlspaces, sewers. The GM tells you if such a
route exists when you enter a structure.
