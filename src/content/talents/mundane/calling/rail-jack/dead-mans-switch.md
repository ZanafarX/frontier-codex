## Effect

**Setup time:** 10 minutes.

You can rig a **dead man's switch** to any explosive device. If you
are Downed, killed, or release the trigger voluntarily, the charge
detonates. The switch can also be set to a specific condition: *"If
that door opens," "If the rope is cut," "If I say the word."*

The setup is visible to anyone observing (you're holding a trigger,
there's a wire), which is the point — it's a deterrent.

## Special

When you hold a Dead Man's Switch in a social encounter, any NPC who
recognizes what it is suffers **−2 dice** to resist your Force
(Intimidation) checks. This is the Rail-Jack's **"nuclear option."**
