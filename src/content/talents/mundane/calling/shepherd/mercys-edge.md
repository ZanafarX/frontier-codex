## Effect

When you deal damage to a living creature, you may choose to deal
**Strain damage instead of HP damage**. This represents precise
strikes to pressure points, temporary disabling rather than killing.

## Special

Creatures reduced to 0 HP this way are **Unconscious rather than
Dying**.
