## Effect

When an ally is **Downed (0 HP, Bleeding Out)**, you may declare a
**Vigil** as a Standard action (Tempo 5). For as long as you maintain
the Vigil (you cannot move, attack, or take other actions), the Downed
ally does not lose HP from Bleeding Out. You are keeping them alive
through sheer will, pressure, and whispered encouragement.

If you are hit, damaged, or forced to move, the Vigil ends.

## Special

If the Downed ally dies while under your Vigil (from a new attack, not
from bleeding), you may **Spark any one of your Memories** as a free
action — the emotional shock focuses you.
