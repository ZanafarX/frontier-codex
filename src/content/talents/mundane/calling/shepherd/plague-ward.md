## Effect

**Resource cost:** 1 hour + materials (Cost Rating 1).

You can establish a **quarantine zone** (one building or campsite).
Within the zone:

- Disease **cannot spread between occupants**
- Existing infections **stabilize** (do not worsen)
- Recovery checks gain **+2 Roll Bonus**

The ward lasts **24 hours** before needing re-establishment.

## Special

In the **Fen** or **Ash Belt**, where environmental disease is
constant, a Plague Ward negates the daily exposure checks for all
occupants.
