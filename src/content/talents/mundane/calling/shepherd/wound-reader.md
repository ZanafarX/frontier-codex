## Effect

By examining a **corpse (10 minutes)** or a living person's **wounds
(1 minute)**, you can determine:

- The weapon type that caused the injury
- The approximate time of the wound
- The physical strength of the attacker (IRON rank, roughly)
- Whether **Psionic effects** were involved

## Special

On a corpse, you can determine the cause of death and time of death
within an hour's accuracy. If the death was caused by a Veil-Born
creature, you identify the creature type.
