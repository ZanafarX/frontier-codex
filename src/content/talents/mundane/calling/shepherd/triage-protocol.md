## Effect

When you perform **First Aid** in combat (Tempo 6, Medicine TN 11),
you may treat **two adjacent characters simultaneously** with a single
action and a single roll. Both receive the full healing benefit.

## Special

Your First Aid is **not limited to once per character per scene** —
you can treat the same character multiple times.
