## Effect

Give a brief speech (requires 1 minute or one Tempo 6 action in
combat). All allies who hear you gain **+2 Roll Bonus** to resist Fear
effects for the rest of the scene.

## Special

If you invoke genuine religious conviction (and the GM agrees it's
sincere), the bonus increases to **+4**.
