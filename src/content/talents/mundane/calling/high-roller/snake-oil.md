## Effect

You can sell worthless or low-quality items at up to **2 Cost Ratings
higher** than their actual value. The buyer won't realize the
deception for **1d6 hours** (or until they use the item, whichever
comes first). When they do, their Disposition toward you drops by
**two steps**.

## Special

If you sell counterfeit Salt (sand mixed with salt dust), fake
ammunition (painted lead), or placebo medicine, the GM should track
when the buyer discovers the fraud. This *will* come back to haunt you.
