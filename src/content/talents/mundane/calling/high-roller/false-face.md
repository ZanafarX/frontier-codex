## Effect

**Resource cost:** 1 Mettle + appropriate clothing/props.

Adopt a false identity for a scene. You gain **+3 Roll Bonus** to all
Deceit checks to maintain the persona and **+2 Roll Bonus** to
Persuasion checks while in character. NPCs who have no reason to
suspect you accept the identity without a check.

## Resist

NPCs with cause for suspicion must beat your **SWAY + Deceit** in an
opposed **Insight** check to see through it.

## Special

The disguise collapses instantly if someone who knows the real person
you're impersonating is present, or if you use any skill or knowledge
the persona wouldn't possess.
