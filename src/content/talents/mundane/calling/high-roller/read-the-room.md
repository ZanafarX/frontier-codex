## Effect

Spend 1 minute observing a social situation. The GM tells you each
significant NPC's **primary motivation** (greed, fear, desire,
loyalty, etc.) and their **current emotional state**.

## Special

Against targets actively concealing their intentions, this requires an
Insight check opposed by their Deceit.
