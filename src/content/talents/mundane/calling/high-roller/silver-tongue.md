## Effect

When you succeed on a Persuasion or Deceit check, you may spend 1
Mettle to improve the result by one "step" of cooperation:

**Hostile → Unfriendly → Neutral → Friendly → Allied**.

## Limit

Cannot be used on the same target more than once per scene.
