## Effect

Improvised weapons (chairs, bottles, chains, shovels, rocks) you wield
gain **+1 WR** (making a chair WR 2 instead of WR 1) and do not suffer
the **Unfamiliar Equipment penalty** (−2 Roll Penalty).

## Special

You can pick up and wield an environmental object as part of an attack
action: no separate Interact action required.
