## Effect

When you and at least one ally attack the same target in the same
**Tick Cycle** (between Count revolutions), the second attacker gains
**+2 Roll Bonus**. If three or more allies attack the same target, the
third and subsequent attackers gain **+3 Roll Bonus**. You must be one
of the participating attackers.

## Limit

Once per scene. The bonus only applies during a single Tick Cycle.
