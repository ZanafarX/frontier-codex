## Effect

You gain **+3 Roll Bonus** to resist Poison, Disease, and Toxin
effects. If you successfully resist a poison, you are **immune to that
specific poison for the rest of the session**.

## Special

You can consume contaminated water and spoiled food without ill effect
(your gut has adapted to the worst the Frontier offers).
