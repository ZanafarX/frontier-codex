## Effect

You can carry **twice the normal load** before becoming Encumbered.
When carrying a **Downed ally**, you move at **full Speed** instead of
half. You can drag heavy objects (mine carts, barricades, unconscious
horses) that would normally require two people.

## Special

You never take Strain from heavy labor. Digging, hauling, and
construction are as natural as breathing.
