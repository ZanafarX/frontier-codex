## Effect

When you take your first Wound in a scene (drop to ½ HP or below), you
may immediately take **one free action** (any action at Weapon Base
Tempo or lower) before the Wounded penalty takes effect. This
represents the adrenaline surge of a body that's been conditioned to
push through pain.

## Limit

Once per scene.
