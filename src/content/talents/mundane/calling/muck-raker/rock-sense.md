## Effect

You can detect structural instability in buildings, mines, bridges,
and natural formations without a check. The GM tells you whether a
structure is safe, questionable, or about to collapse.

In **underground environments**, you cannot become lost and you
automatically detect gas pockets, flooded passages, and unstable
ceilings before the party encounters them.
