## Effect

Using available materials (earth, rubble, furniture, crates), you
create an improvised fortification in your current position. This
provides **Hard Cover (+4 TN)** to anyone behind it for the rest of
the scene. The fortification is stationary and can absorb **10 HP** of
damage before being destroyed.

## Special

If you have a **shovel or pickaxe**, reduce the Tempo to **5** in
combat (5 minutes out of combat).
