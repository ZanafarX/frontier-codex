## Effect

When you fire a shotgun at **Close range or Point-Blank**, you may
spend 1 Mettle to fire both barrels simultaneously. The attack uses a
single roll but deals **double WR damage** on hit. The weapon is empty
afterward.

## Limit

Only works with double-barreled weapons (Coach Gun, Sawed-Off). The
weapon must have 2 loaded barrels.
