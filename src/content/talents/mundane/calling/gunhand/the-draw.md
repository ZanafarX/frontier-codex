## Effect

Combine **Draw Weapon** (Tempo 2) and **Steady Shot** (Weapon Base
Tempo) into a single action at **Tempo = Weapon Base** (no added Draw
cost). This does not trigger the **Exposed** condition.

## Special

If used as your first action in combat, gain **+1 Roll Bonus** to the
attack.
