## Effect

You have perfect recall for faces, names, and bounties. Once you've
seen a wanted poster or been given a target's description, you
automatically recognize them regardless of disguise (though magical
disguises may require an Insight check).

## Special

Gain **+2 Roll Bonus** to track a specific bounty target.
