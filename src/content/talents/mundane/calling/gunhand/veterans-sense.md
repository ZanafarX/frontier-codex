## Effect

You cannot be **Surprised** (per Ch.5 §II). Even if you fail your
Initiative roll, you still act at **Tick 6** without the Surprised
condition (you can spend Mettle on Reactions, you are not Flat-Footed).

## Special

The first time per session an NPC lies to you in conversation, the GM
tells you they're lying; you don't know *what* the lie is, only that
it was one.
