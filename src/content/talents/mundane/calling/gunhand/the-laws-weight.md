## Effect

When you make a Force (Intimidation) check, you may invoke the
authority of whatever badge or warrant you carry. Targets who respect
that authority suffer **−2 dice (Pool Penalty)** to resist.

## Special

Has no effect on Dust Vultures, outlaws with nothing to lose, or
anyone who doesn't recognize your authority.
