## Effect

When mounted, you gain the standard **+1 Step** to melee damage against
unmounted targets (per Ch.5 §XIII) and additionally:

- Your mounted **Charge damage bonus increases from +2 to +4**.
- You may make a melee attack as part of a Charge **without using a
  separate action** (the attack resolves at the Charge's Tempo).

## Special

When your mount is killed or panics, you may dismount cleanly: no
QUICK + Ride check required, no falling damage.
