## Effect

Lock eyes with a single target within Near range. Make an **opposed
Force check** vs. the target's **GRIT + Resilience**. If you win,
choose one:

- The target **drops their weapon**.
- The target **backs down and withdraws**.
- The target **freezes (Stunned for 3 Ticks)**.

This is not supernatural — it's the weight of a killer's gaze.

## Resist

The opposed roll is the resist (GRIT + Resilience vs. your Force).
Additionally, targets with Mettle remaining may spend **1 Mettle** to
negate the effect entirely (they stare right back).
