## Effect

**Trigger:** You are targeted by a psionic attack.

Roll ECHO + Channel vs. the attacker's original Channeling Sum. If you win,
the attack affects the original caster instead of you, using their own
Margin for effect determination.

## Steps

+1 additional protected ally per Step (you can defend yourself plus N
adjacent allies in the same Reaction).

## Requirement

**Cannot reflect Rank 5 Miracles**: those exceed the Talent's scope.

## Special

The reflected attack uses the caster's original roll, so a Critical they
landed on you becomes a Critical against them. This is karmic geometry.
Subject to the Bastion Soul Reaction Convention.
