## Effect

Touch a creature or object. Any **Rank 1 psionic effect** currently affecting
it ends immediately. The target is also immune to Rank 1 psionic effects for
**1 minute**.

## Steps

+1 minute duration per Step.

## Special

Unwilling targets require a melee attack roll to touch. **Cannot affect
higher-rank effects**: see the Tier-Removal Hierarchy in the discipline
overview.
