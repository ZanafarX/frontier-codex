## Effect

For the rest of the scene, allies within Close range (5m) of you gain
**+2 Roll Bonus** to resist psionic effects.

## Steps

+1 Roll Bonus per Step (maximum **+4** total from Steps).

## Special

You must remain conscious for the effect to persist. If you are knocked
unconscious or Stunned beyond 1 round, the Shield drops.
