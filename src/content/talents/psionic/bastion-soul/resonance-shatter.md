## Effect

Target one Psionic creature within Near range. Their current Resonance is
destroyed: they lose whatever Resonance they have built and cannot establish
new Resonance until their next Long Rest.

## Resist

ECHO + Focus vs. your Channeling Sum negates the effect entirely.

## Steps

+1 additional target per Step.

## Special

This is surgically devastating. A Psy-Slinger relying on free Ghost Bullets
via Resonance Echo suddenly has to pay full Strain for every shot. A
Mindweaver maintaining cheap Veil of Silence echoes is suddenly exposed.
Resonance Shatter forces Psionics to fight at full cost.
