## Effect

Target one Psionic creature within Near range. Their connection to the
Current is sealed for **1 hour**:

- They cannot Channel any Talents (of any Rank)
- They cannot use Resonance
- They cannot use Bleed Dice
- Active psionic effects they are maintaining end immediately
- Their Erosion track is frozen (cannot increase or decrease)

## Resist

ECHO + Focus vs. your Channeling Sum negates the effect entirely. Once
sealed, the target can attempt to break the seal once per hour (same check,
cumulative −1 per attempt).

## Steps

+1 hour duration per Step.

## Special

This is Thread-Cutter Round (Psy-Slinger R4) in Talent form, but more
thorough — it also freezes Erosion and blocks Bleed Dice. Against enemy
Psionics, this is the nuclear option short of a Miracle. **Per the
Tier-Removal Hierarchy, Seal the Weave severs ALL ranks of effects on the
target while active** — even though the Talent itself is R3. The
single-target scope is the trade-off.
