## Effect

For the rest of the scene, your defensive capabilities scale with the
number of allies who are Wounded or Downed within Near range:

- **For each Wounded ally:** +1 AR and +1 Defense
- **For each Downed ally:** +2 AR and +2 Defense
- **For each dead ally (this scene):** +3 AR and +3 Defense, and you become
  immune to Fear and Suppression

**Bonus Cap:** Total bonuses from this Talent cap at **+6 AR / +6 Defense**
regardless of fallen ally count.

Additionally: while under the Aegis, your Kinetic Dampener's damage
reduction **doubles**.

## Steps

Allies within Close range also gain half your Aegis bonuses (round down)
per Step.

## Special

This is the Bastion Soul's tragedy Talent. You become stronger as your
allies fall. The worse things get, the harder you are to kill, but you're
watching the people you swore to protect bleed out around you. The Aegis
burns brightest when everything is already lost.
