## Effect

All allies within Near range gain the following for the rest of the scene:

- **+2 Roll Bonus** to resist all psionic effects
- **Immunity to Rank 1 psionic Talents** (they simply fizzle)
- **+1 AR** against Psionic attacks that deal physical damage (telekinetic
  strikes, Boil Blood feedback, etc.)

## Steps

+1 Roll Bonus per Step (max **+4** total from Steps).

## Special

The Mantle is visible: a faint gray shimmer around each protected ally,
like heat haze. Enemy Psionics can see who is protected and adjust tactics
accordingly. The Mantle ends if you are knocked unconscious.
