## Effect

For **1 hour**, you are invisible to all psionic detection:

- You cannot be targeted by Surface Read, Thread Sight, Psychic Anchor, or
  any Talent that requires sensing a psychic signature
- Threadrippers and other Current-sensing Veil-Born cannot detect you
- You do not appear in the Psionic "web" that other Psionics can sense
- Your Channeling does not feed the Tension Pool during this period

## Steps

+1 hour duration per Step.

## Special

You appear as a "dead spot" to psionic senses — a place where the Current
doesn't flow. Seers describe it as looking at a shadow that has no source.
This cuts both ways: being invisible to psionic detection means you can't
be helped by psionic buffs either (Probability Spike, Shared Foresight,
etc.).
