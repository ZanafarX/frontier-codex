## Effect

You bleed onto the ground and consecrate a Close range area centered on
yourself. The area becomes resistant to the Current for **8 hours**:

- The **Tension Pool cannot increase** from events within the area
- Channeling within the area costs **+1 Strain** above normal (the Current
  is harder to reach)
- Veil-Born creatures entering the area must make ECHO + Resilience (TN 11)
  or be repelled (cannot enter for 1 minute; can retry)

## Steps

+4 hours duration per Step.

## Special

This functions similarly to the Shepherd's mundane Sanctified Ground but
is more powerful (it taxes Channeling and the Tension Pool cost is lower —
Strain instead of a Salt Pouch). The trade-off is the **1 HP cost** and the
Erosion risk inherent in any Psionic Talent. The HP cost cannot be reduced
or absorbed — the ritual requires real blood on real ground.
