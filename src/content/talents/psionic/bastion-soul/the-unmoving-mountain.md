## Effect

For **30 Ticks** (approximately 3 rounds), you become **Invulnerable**:

- Take **0 damage** from all sources (physical and psionic)
- Cannot be moved, knocked prone, or transformed
- Immune to all conditions and psionic effects
- Cannot be targeted by Mindweaver Talents

## Requirement

You cannot Move or Attack while Invulnerable. You can only:

- Speak
- Use Kinetic Dampener (R1) to protect adjacent allies
- End the effect early (free action)

## Special

**The Mountain's Shadow:** Allies standing directly behind you (within 2m)
gain **Hard Cover** (+4 TN to be hit) for the duration.

**Aftermath:** When the effect ends, you are **Exhausted** (−2 dice to all
physical actions) until you rest.
