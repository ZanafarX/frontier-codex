## Effect

For **10 Ticks**, you become an active hazard to the Current:

- Any psionic Talent that targets you automatically fails. The caster takes
  **3 Strain** as their power rebounds.
- Any psionic effect that enters your Close range area is destroyed (ongoing
  effects on allies near you are purged, beneficial or not)
- You gain **+3 AR** against all sources (you are unnaturally dense: bullets
  flatten, blades turn)
- Your melee attacks deal **+2 damage** (your fists carry the weight of
  denied reality)

## Steps

+1 Strain to rebounding casters per Step (max **6 Strain** total).

## Special

You are a walking anti-psionic weapon. Standing near you purges ALL psionic
effects, including beneficial ones from allies. A Flesh Shaper's Iron Skin,
a Seer's Probability Spike, a Psy-Slinger's Tracer Round, all stripped away
if the targets are within Close range. Coordinate with your party.
