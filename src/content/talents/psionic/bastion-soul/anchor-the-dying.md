## Effect

**Trigger:** A creature within Touch range reaches 0 HP.

The target does not begin Bleeding Out. Instead, they are stabilized at 0 HP
for **1 minute**. They are conscious but cannot take actions. If not healed
within the minute, Bleeding Out begins normally.

## Steps

+1 minute of stabilization per Step.

## Special

You are physically holding their soul in their body. You must maintain
contact — if you let go or are moved away, the effect ends. This pairs
naturally with a Flesh Shaper's healing: the Bastion Soul holds the dying
steady while the healer works. Subject to the Bastion Soul Reaction
Convention.
