## Effect

Target one creature within Near range. They become unable to use psionic
movement abilities (teleportation, phasing, flight from psionic sources)
for **1 minute**.

## Resist

ECHO + Focus vs. your Channeling Sum. On success, the target shrugs the
effect off entirely.

## Steps

+1 minute duration per Step.

## Special

Also grounds **Ethereal creatures**, forcing them to remain material for the
duration. Pairs naturally with Anchor Shot (Psy-Slinger R3) for double-anchor
ethereal lockdown.
