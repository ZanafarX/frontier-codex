## Effect

**Trigger:** You are hit by a psionic attack that deals damage or Strain.

Reduce the incoming psionic damage or Strain by your **ECHO + IRON** (sum
of both Attributes). If this reduces the effect to 0 or less, the attack
is negated entirely.

## Steps

+2 additional absorption per Step.

## Special

Unlike Kinetic Dampener (which blocks physical damage), Absorb blocks
**psionic** damage specifically: Phantom Pain, Boil Blood, Psychic Venom,
and similar effects. The two Talents complement each other: Dampener for
bullets, Absorb for the Current. Subject to the Bastion Soul Reaction
Convention.
