## Effect

For the rest of the scene, all Psionic creatures within Close range of you
suffer **−1 die** (Pool Penalty) to all Channel checks. Additionally,
Veil-Born creatures within the aura take **1 damage** at the start of each
of their turns (the null field burns them).

## Steps

−1 additional die per Step (max **−3**).

## Special

The aura is perceptible: Psionics feel a painful static, like needles in
the teeth, when near you. Non-Psionics feel nothing. The aura does not
affect mundane actions, only Channeling.
