## Effect

You create a dome of compressed time at Close range radius, lasting
**5 Ticks**. Inside the dome, time flows normally. Outside, from the
perspective of those inside, the world appears to slow. Any creature or
projectile entering the dome from outside ages rapidly:

- **Projectiles** (bullets, arrows) rust and disintegrate before reaching
  you. All ranged attacks from outside the dome fail automatically.
- **Creatures entering the dome take 6 damage** (ignoring AR) as their
  bodies experience years of aging in a heartbeat. Living creatures that
  survive are **Stunned for 3 Ticks** from the disorientation.
- **Inanimate objects** thrown or pushed into the dome crumble to dust.

## Steps

+1 entry damage per Step (max **9 damage**, R4 ceiling).

## Special

Allies inside the dome can fire *out*: projectiles leaving the dome are
unaffected (time compression is one-directional). This makes the dome a
perfect defensive position: you can shoot out, they can't shoot in. The
limitation is size (Close range) and duration, and the 2 Erosion cost.
