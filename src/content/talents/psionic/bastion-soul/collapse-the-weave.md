## Effect

All psionic effects of **Rank 1–4** within Near range end immediately. All
creatures with psionic abilities within range must make a Resist check (see
below) or be **Stunned** as their connection to the Current is temporarily
severed. Stunned creatures also take **1 Strain**.

## Resist

Affected Psionic creatures roll ECHO + Focus vs. your Channeling Sum.
Success negates the Stun and Strain entirely; failure applies both.

## Steps

+1 additional Strain on Stunned creatures per Step.

## Special

This affects allies as well as enemies. **Your own ongoing effects also end.**
Per the Tier-Removal Hierarchy, this Talent does NOT affect Rank 5
Miracles: those continue.
