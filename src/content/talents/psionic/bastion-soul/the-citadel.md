## Effect

You create a dome of absolute psychic protection at Near range, centered
on yourself, lasting **1 minute**:

- **No Psionic Talent of any Rank** can affect any creature inside the dome
  from outside (including Miracles)
- No Psionic Talent activated inside the dome can affect anything outside
- All creatures inside gain **+4 Roll Bonus** to resist Fear, psionic
  intrusion, and mental effects
- **Erosion does not increase** for any creature inside the dome from any
  source
- The dome is **physically impenetrable** — nothing passes through the
  barrier in either direction (no bullets, no creatures, no sound). It is
  a sealed space.

## Special

The Citadel is a time-out. A moment of perfect safety in a world that has
forgotten what safety means. The dome shimmers faintly gray and is warm
to the touch from the inside — the warmth of being held, of being
protected, of being told that nothing can reach you here. When it drops,
the cold rushes back in. But for one minute, you were safe.

**The Citadel is the only Talent in the game that blocks Rank 5 effects
from outside.** Even Reality Anchor's "all ranks" capability only ends
active effects; The Citadel actively *prevents* Miracles from crossing
the dome.
