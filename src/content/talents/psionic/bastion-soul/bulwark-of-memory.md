## Effect

Touch a target and choose one of their Core Memories. That Memory is
permanently reinforced:

- It cannot be burned by Erosion overflow (the Break still occurs, but
  the target chooses a different Memory)
- It cannot be erased by Memory Hole or other Mindweaver effects
- It cannot be suppressed by Memory Palace Siege
- The Memory's Spark effect (Blaze or Ember) can be used **twice per
  session** instead of once

## Steps

The protected Memory also grants **+1 to the Mental Defense Bonus** per
Step (stacking with the normal Bulwark table).

## Requirement

You can maintain a maximum of **3 Bulwarks simultaneously** (across any
number of allies). Creating a 4th requires dropping one.

## Special

Duration is **permanent** until the Bastion Soul dies or voluntarily removes
the protection. If you die, the protection fades over 24 hours. This is the
Bastion Soul's most emotionally significant Talent — you are personally
guarding someone's identity. Allies who have benefited from your Bulwark
know what you did for them.
