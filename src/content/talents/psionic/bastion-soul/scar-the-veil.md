## Effect

Touch a Thin Place or Veil breach. After the initial Channeling roll,
sustain Channeling for 10 minutes. On completion, you permanently damage
the Veil's ability to thin in this exact location:

- The Thin Place is sealed **permanently**. It can never thin again at
  this exact location.
- Veil-Born creatures cannot manifest within Far range of the scar for
  **1 month**.
- The Tension Pool's maximum capacity increases by **1 permanently** while
  the party is within the scarred area.

## Steps

+1 month Veil-Born exclusion per Step.

## Special

The scar is visible: a permanent discoloration in the air, like a bruise
on reality. Other Psionics who see it feel discomfort. The Circle of Ash
has mixed feelings about Veil Scars: they are effective, but they are also
wounds upon wounds. Some argue scarring the Veil is no better than tearing
it. The debate is unresolved.
