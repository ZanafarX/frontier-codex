## Effect

Touch a willing ally. Transfer one condition or debuff from them to yourself.
You can absorb:

- **Suppressed**, **Blinded** (temporary), **Stunned**, or **Prone** conditions
- Up to **3 Strain**
- A **Grievous Wound** (you suffer the wound instead of them)
- A psionic effect currently affecting them (you become the new target,
  inheriting remaining duration)

## Steps

+1 additional ally per Step (absorb from multiple sources in one cast).

## Special

You take the full weight of whatever you absorb. If you take a Grievous
Wound, it's real — your body suffers the Broken Bone, the Internal Bleeding,
the Concussion. The Bastion Soul's role is to be the one who can bear it.
Iron endures.
