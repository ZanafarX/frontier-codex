## Effect

Touch an ally. Remove the **Frightened** or **Suppressed** condition from
them if caused by a psionic or supernatural source. The ally also gains
**+2 Roll Bonus** to resist Fear and Suppression from supernatural sources
for the rest of the scene.

## Steps

+1 additional ally per Step (must touch each).

## Special

Iron Calm does not work against mundane fear (a gun pointed at your face is
still scary). It specifically counters psionic fear: Fearmonger, The Fatal
Reading, Mass Hysteria, Veil-Born terror auras.
