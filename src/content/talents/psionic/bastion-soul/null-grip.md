## Effect

For **1 minute**, anything you physically hold or touch becomes temporarily
immune to psionic effects:

- A weapon you hold cannot be Disarmed by telekinesis
- A person you're touching gains **+2 Roll Bonus** to resist all psionic
  effects
- An object you're carrying cannot be affected by Talents that target objects
- A rope you hold cannot be severed by psionic means

## Steps

+1 minute duration per Step.

## Special

This is the bodyguard Talent. Hold someone's hand and they're shielded. Hold
the door closed and a Mindweaver can't telekinetically open it. The
limitation is your hands — you can only touch so many things at once.
