## Effect

Touch one object. Ward it against psionic tampering for **24 hours**. The
warded object:

- Cannot be affected by telekinesis, Fleshcraft (if organic), or any Talent
  that targets objects
- Cannot be phased through (Ghost Bullet cannot pass through a warded wall
  or door)
- Provides **+1 Roll Bonus** to Focus checks for anyone holding it

## Steps

+24 hours duration per Step.

## Special

The object feels heavier, denser, more *present*. Warded iron in particular
has a dull gray sheen Psionics find uncomfortable to look at. Warding a Salt
Pouch extends its effective duration — a warded pouch doesn't lose potency
as quickly.
