## Effect

You create an aura with a Near range radius (10m) centered on yourself. For
the rest of the scene (or until you end it):

- **No Rank 1–4 psionic Talents** can be activated within the zone
- Active psionic effects are **suppressed** (resume when the target leaves
  the zone)
- Veil-Born creatures suffer **−2 dice** (Pool Penalty) to all actions

## Steps

+5m radius per Step.

## Requirement

You cannot use other Psionic Talents while maintaining the Null Zone. Your
Speed is halved.

## Special

**Rank 5 Miracles still function within the Null Zone** — those bend reality
at a level beyond the discipline. Use Reality Anchor (R4) for total psionic
shutdown including Miracles, or The Citadel (R5) to actively block Miracles
from outside.
