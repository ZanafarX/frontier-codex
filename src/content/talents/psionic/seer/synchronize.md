## Effect

Link your Count position with a willing ally's. For the rest of the scene,
you share the same Tick position on The Count. When either of you acts,
you both act simultaneously.

Actions can be coordinated perfectly — flanking, simultaneous shots,
cover-and-advance.

## Steps

+1 additional ally per Step (all share the same Tick position).

## Special

The linked group resolves actions simultaneously, gaining the benefits of
perfect coordination: **flanking bonuses apply automatically when
applicable**, and **combined attacks against the same target gain +1 Roll
Bonus each**. If the group becomes separated beyond Near range, the link
breaks.
