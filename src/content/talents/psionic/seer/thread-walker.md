## Effect

You vanish and reappear instantly at any location you can see within
**Far range**.

**Combat Benefits:**

- Does not trigger Overwatch or free attacks
- Can appear in **Flanking position** (−2 TN for allies)
- Ignores all terrain obstacles

## Steps

+1 additional "hop" per Step (can teleport multiple times in sequence).

## Special

You can bring **one willing, touched creature** with you at +1 Tempo
(Tempo 4 instead of 3). Anchor Shot (Psy-Slinger R3) and Weight of
Reality (Bastion Soul R2) can prevent Thread-Walker from initiating —
the silver thread won't take you anywhere.
