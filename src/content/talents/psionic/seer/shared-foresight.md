## Effect

Touch an ally. For the rest of the scene, they gain the benefits of your
**Danger Sense** Talent (cannot be Surprised; freeze before traps).

## Steps

+1 additional ally per Step (must touch each).

## Special

If you don't have Danger Sense, you can still grant a lesser version:
**+2 Roll Bonus to Initiative checks** for the affected allies.
