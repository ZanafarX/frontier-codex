## Effect

For the rest of the scene, whenever any creature within Near range rolls
dice, you see the result **1 Tick before it happens**. You may choose to:

- **Warn an ally:** Grant **+1 Roll Bonus** to their roll (you called
  out *"Duck!"* or *"Aim left!"*)
- **Taunt an enemy:** Impose **−1 die** (Pool Penalty) on their roll
  (your confidence unnerves them, a Fear effect)
- **Do nothing:** Conserve your focus

You may only intervene in **one roll per Tick**.

## Steps

+1 additional roll intervention per Tick per Step.

## Special

This is the Seer at the card table: not playing the game, but reading
every hand. The Talent's name references the legendary poker hand held
by the dead; Seers joke that they've already seen that hand too.
