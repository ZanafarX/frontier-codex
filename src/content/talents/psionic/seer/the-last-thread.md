## Effect

**Trigger:** Your Vitality reaches your Death Threshold.

You see the thread of your life about to snap — and you seize it. You
do not die. Instead:

- Your Vitality stabilizes at **1** (you are Downed but not Bleeding Out)
- You gain **1 final Action** before collapsing — any Action, at any
  Tempo, with **+4 Roll Bonus**
- If this Action **kills an enemy**, you are stabilized automatically
  (no Medicine check needed)
- If this Action **saves an ally's life** (stabilizing them, removing a
  lethal threat), you recover to **half Vitality**
- If this Action does **neither**, you collapse and begin Bleeding Out
  normally

## Requirement

**Can only be used once, ever.** The burned Memory is the price of
admission to the one future where you survive.

## Special

**The Price Beyond Memory:** You have cheated death. Death remembers.
The GM adds **3 dice to the Tension Pool** immediately. Something from
the Veil felt you grab the thread — and it is now looking for you
specifically.

A Seer who has used The Last Thread is **marked** — other Seers see a
faint, frayed end trailing behind them where the thread was cut and
reattached. They call it *"the scar on the loom."*

Per the Miracle Reaction Rule, the Memory cost is the entire cost — no
Mettle is required despite this being a Reaction.
