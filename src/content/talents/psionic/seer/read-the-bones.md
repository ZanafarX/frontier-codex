## Effect

Perform a reading for yourself or a willing subject. The GM provides
**one** of the following:

- A symbolic image related to the subject's near future (next session)
- A warning about a specific danger the subject will face
- A cryptic clue related to the current plot

## Steps

+1 additional piece of information per Step.

## Requirement

Bone dice, tarot cards, tea leaves, or similar divination tools (your
Focus).

## Special

Unlike Omen Reading (which asks specific yes/no questions), Read the
Bones provides **unsolicited narrative information**. The GM has creative
license with the symbolism. This Talent is the Seer's primary tool for
generating plot hooks and foreshadowing.
