## Effect

Target one creature within Near range. They see a vision of their own
violent death.

## Resist

ECHO + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum.

- **Failure:** Target is **Paralyzed** (cannot act) for **5 Ticks**.
  Afterward, they are **Suppressed** for the scene.
- **Success:** Target is only **Suppressed** for the scene.

## Steps

+3 Ticks of Paralysis per Step.

## Special

If the target actually dies in the manner you described within the
scene, the GM awards **+1 XP**. This is the Seer's incentive to
describe specific, achievable deaths rather than vague threats.
