## Effect

Retroactively declare that you performed a minor preparatory action
earlier in the day that is now relevant.

**Examples:** *"I bought a length of rope in town," "I memorized the guard
rotation while we ate breakfast," "I left a knife hidden under the table
during the first meeting."*

## Steps

+1 additional retroactive prep element per Step (each must be plausible).

## Requirement

The preparation must be plausible given your skills and resources. The GM
has veto power over implausible claims.

## Special

This is the Seer's most beloved utility Talent: it lets them *always
seem prepared*. Whether they actually foresaw the need or are
retroactively editing causality is an open question even among the
Circle of Ash.
