## Effect

**Trigger:** Any moment, any trigger: you see something you cannot
accept.

You grab the thread of time and pull. The scene resets to the state it
was in at the **start of the previous Tick Cycle** (approximately 10
seconds ago).

**What Resets:**

- All damage taken is undone
- All ammo spent is returned
- All positions reset
- The dead stand back up
- Spoken words are unspoken

**What Remains:**

- Only you remember what happened
- Your Memory is still burned
- You still take **1d6 Strain** immediately
- Any Erosion you gained remains

## Special

You may share the memory with **one ally** by touching them during the
Rewind. They also remember, and take **1d3 Strain**.

Per the Miracle Reaction Rule, the Memory cost is the entire cost: no
Mettle is required despite this being a Reaction.
