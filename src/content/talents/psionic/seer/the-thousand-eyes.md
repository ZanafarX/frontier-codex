## Effect

For **1 minute**, you achieve total temporal omniscience within Near
range:

- You see all possible futures simultaneously
- All attacks against you **automatically miss** (you've already moved
  to the only position where they can't reach you)
- All your attacks **automatically hit** (you fire at the only moment
  the target is vulnerable)
- You **cannot be Surprised, Flanked, Disrupted, or affected by any
  effect you don't choose to be affected by**
- You can take **2 Actions per turn** instead of 1

## Special

**The Price Beyond Memory:** When the minute ends, you are **Blinded for
1 hour** (your eyes have seen too much). You take **2d6 Strain** (excess
converts to Erosion). You experience a splitting headache that imposes
**−2 dice** (Pool Penalty) to all actions for **24 hours**.

The Thousand Eyes is sensory overload on a cosmic scale. You see your
own death — many versions of it. You see your allies' deaths. You see
the Veil opening. The information doesn't go away when the Talent ends;
it fades slowly over days, leaving afterimages of futures that may
never happen. Many Seers who use this Talent develop **permanent
temporal dysphoria**.
