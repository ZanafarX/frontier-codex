## Effect

Grant an ally (or yourself) within Near range a flash of total
battlefield awareness. For their **next turn only**, the target:

- Cannot be **Surprised, Flanked, or targeted by Rear Attacks**
- Gains **+3 dice** (Pool Bonus) to their next roll
- Knows the **exact HP, Defense, and Mettle** of every enemy within Near
  range
- Knows **which enemy will act next** on The Count and what action they
  intend

## Steps

+1 additional ally per Step.

## Special

The clarity fades after one turn. The information overload causes **1
Strain** to each affected creature (the mind isn't meant to see this much
at once). Worth it.
