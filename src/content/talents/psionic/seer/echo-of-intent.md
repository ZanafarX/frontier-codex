## Effect

Choose one target within Near range. For the rest of the scene, you know
what action they intend to take **1 Tick before** they take it. In combat,
this grants you **+2 Roll Bonus** to Defense against that target's attacks.
Outside combat, you can anticipate their next statement, gesture, or
decision.

## Steps

+1 additional target per Step.

## Special

Does not work against creatures with **no intent** (Hollows, mindless
Veil-Born, constructs). Works normally against Psionics and intelligent
enemies.
