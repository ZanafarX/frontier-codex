## Effect

For the next minute, you see the "threads" connecting people, objects,
and places. You can perceive:

- **Emotional connections** between people
- **Objects of significance** to a person
- The **general direction** of important locations
- **Lies** (targets suffer **−4 dice** to Deceit against you)

## Steps

+1 minute duration per Step.

## Special

Thread Sight does not penetrate Bastion Soul Null Auras (those appear as
*absences* in the threadwork). Highly Veil-touched objects glow with a
violet sheen visible only through Thread Sight.
