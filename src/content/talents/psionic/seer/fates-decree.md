## Effect

Make a single declarative statement about reality, and it becomes true.
The statement must be:

- **Specific** (not abstract)
- **Achievable within the laws of physics** (the Current bends fate, not
  matter)
- **Limited in scope**

**Examples:**

- *"The next bullet fired in this room misses."*
- *"The rope holding the chandelier snaps."*
- *"The guard changes his mind about checking this room."*
- *"The storm arrives in the next five minutes."*

## Requirement

The statement **cannot directly kill, resurrect, or teleport**. It
manipulates circumstance, not substance.

## Special

Fate's Decree is the Seer equivalent of a wish, and like all wishes,
the universe interprets it with **hostile literalism**. *"The next
bullet misses"* might mean the shot goes wide and kills a bystander.
*"The storm arrives"* might bring a Veil Rupture with it. The GM is
encouraged to honor the decree but introduce unforeseen consequences.
