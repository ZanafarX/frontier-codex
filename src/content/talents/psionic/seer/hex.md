## Effect

Curse a target within Near range for the rest of the scene. Whenever the
target rolls, **if any die shows a 1**, they suffer a minor complication:

- Weapons jam (Tempo 4 to clear)
- Footing slips (−2 Speed until next turn)
- An item falls from their person (GM's choice)

## Resist

ECHO + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum negates.

## Steps

+1 Strain to the target per triggered complication per Step (the target
also takes Strain when the curse bites).

## Special

The Hex is visible to those who know what to look for: a faint violet
haze clings to the target. Other Psionics can identify a Hexed creature
with an Awareness check (TN 11).
