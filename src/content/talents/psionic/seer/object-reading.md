## Effect

Touch an object and see a vision of the **last intense emotion or violence**
associated with it. Vision quality scales with the **Margin** of your
Channeling roll:

| Margin | Vision Quality |
| :----: | :---- |
| 0–2 | Vague impressions (fear, anger, love) |
| 3–5 | Clear images (faces, locations) |
| 6–8 | Full scenes with audio |
| 9+ | Complete understanding of context |

## Steps

+1 significant event further back in time per Step.

## Special

Also called **Psychometry**. Hindsight (R1) reads location memory; Object
Reading reads object memory. The two complement each other: a knife from
a murder reads under both, but reveals different details.
