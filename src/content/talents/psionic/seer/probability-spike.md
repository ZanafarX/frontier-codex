## Effect

Choose an ally within Near range (can be yourself). Their next roll this
scene gains **+2 dice** (Pool Bonus) — raw power, not accuracy.

## Steps

+1 die per Step.

## Special

Distinct from Fortune's Favor: **Probability Spike adds raw dice ceiling;
Fortune's Favor adds selection consistency.** Both can be cast on the same
target across different rolls but **cannot stack on a single roll**.
