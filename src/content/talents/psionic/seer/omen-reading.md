## Effect

Ask the GM **one yes/no question** about the immediate future (next hour).
The GM answers truthfully, though the answer may be cryptic.

**Examples:** *"Will we be ambushed on this road?" "Is the Sheriff lying?"
"Does this mine contain ghost-rock?"*

## Steps

+1 follow-up question per Step.

## Special

Unlike Read the Bones (which provides unsolicited symbolic narrative),
Omen Reading answers specific questions. The GM may answer in cryptic
imagery if the literal answer would spoil too much.
