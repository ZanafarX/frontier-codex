## Effect

You remove yourself from the flow of time. For **1 minute of subjective
time**:

- The world around you is **frozen**. Nothing moves, decays, burns, or
  changes.
- You can walk through the frozen moment, observing everything from any
  angle
- You **cannot interact** with frozen objects or creatures (your hands
  pass through them)
- You can plan, think, and communicate with the GM about what you observe

When the minute ends, you return to the timestream at the exact position
you occupied when you left.

**The Gift:** Before returning, you may choose to share what you observed
with up to **3 allies** (telepathic flash). They each take **1 Strain**
from the temporal dislocation but gain the tactical information.

## Special

**You age 1 year during the minute outside time.** This is cumulative: a
Seer who uses this Talent frequently ages prematurely. At the GM's
discretion, repeated use may begin to cause visible aging (gray hair,
wrinkles, joint pain). Time always collects its debts.
