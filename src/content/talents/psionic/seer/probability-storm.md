## Effect

For **1 minute**, all rolls made within the affected Near range area
(allies and enemies alike) have their dice behave erratically: after
rolling, **each creature must swap their highest die with their lowest
die** before calculating their sum.

This dramatically reduces the effectiveness of large dice pools and
makes outcomes unpredictable.

**You may exempt your allies from the effect at the time of casting.**

## Steps

+1 minute duration per Step.

## Special

In a Probability Storm, skilled characters perform worse than expected
and unskilled characters perform better than expected; the playing
field levels violently. Use this when the enemy has superior numbers and
skills. The Storm is visible: coins spin endlessly, compasses whirl,
dice thrown nearby land on edges.
