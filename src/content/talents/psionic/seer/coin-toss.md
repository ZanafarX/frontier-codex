## Effect

Before any binary outcome is determined (a choice between two options, a
yes/no decision, left or right at a fork), you know which option leads to
the better outcome **for you** in the immediate future (next 10 minutes).

"Better" is subjective — the Current shows survival, not morality.

## Steps

+10 minutes time horizon per Step.

## Special

This is not infallible. If both options lead to roughly equivalent
outcomes, you get a feeling of indifference. If both options are terrible,
you know the *less* terrible one.
