## Effect

For the rest of the scene, you become a tactical oracle:

- At the start of each **Tick Cycle**, the GM tells you what every enemy
  intends to do (target, action, general strategy)
- You may freely share this information with allies (no action
  required — telepathic impression)
- All allies who receive this information gain **+1 die** (Pool Bonus)
  to all rolls for that Tick Cycle

## Steps

+1 die to the Pool Bonus per Step (max **+3**).

## Special

This is the ultimate support Talent. You're not fighting — you're
directing the fight. The tactical advantage of perfect information is
staggering. However, **enemies who realize you're the source of the
coordination will prioritize you**. Intelligent enemies may begin acting
unpredictably (GM may declare that certain enemies "resist" the web,
making erratic choices to throw off your predictions).
