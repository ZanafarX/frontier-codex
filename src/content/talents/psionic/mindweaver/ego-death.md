## Effect

Target one creature within Near range. They lose all sense of identity
for **1 minute**. They don't know their name, their allegiance, their
goals, or their relationships. They can still act (self-preservation
instinct remains), but they will not fight for a cause, protect a
person, or pursue an objective. They default to **passive confusion**.

## Resist

ECHO + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum negates. **Targets with 5
intact Memories gain +2 to resist** (the Bulwark protects them).

## Steps

+1 minute duration per Step.

## Special

When the effect ends, the target remembers the experience. It is
**deeply traumatic**: **1 Strain** from the psychic aftershock.
Characters who have experienced Ego Death often develop an obsessive
need to reaffirm their identity afterward.

Soul Anchor (BS R3) blocks Ego Death entirely; Anchor reaches R1–R3
identity attacks.
