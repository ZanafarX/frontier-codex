## Effect

Speak through a target's mouth. The target speaks your words in their
own voice, with their mannerisms. They are aware they are speaking but
cannot stop. Lasts for up to **1 minute or 50 words, whichever comes
first**.

## Resist

ECHO + Focus vs. your Channeling Sum to clamp their jaw shut (ends
effect immediately).

## Steps

+30 seconds duration per Step.

## Special

Useful for delivering messages, issuing false orders, or framing someone
for treasonous statements. The target's allies may not realize the words
aren't genuine unless they succeed on **Insight vs. your Channeling
Sum**.
