## Effect

You sense the target's current emotional state and **surface thoughts**
(what they're actively thinking about right now) within Near range.

## Steps

+1 specific detail per Step (a name, a fear, an intention).

## Special

Target is unaware they've been read **unless they have ECHO 3+**, in
which case they feel "watched." Iron Will (BS R1) blocks Surface Read
entirely — the mental fortress is opaque to surface-level reads.
