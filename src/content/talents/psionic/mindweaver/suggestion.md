## Effect

Give the target within Near range a single instruction of up to one
sentence. The instruction must be something the target *could*
rationalize as reasonable: *"Drop your weapon," "Walk outside," "Tell
me who hired you."*

The target carries out the instruction on their next turn.

## Resist

ECHO + Focus vs. your Channeling Sum negates. **Targets ordered to do
something directly harmful to themselves or their closest allies gain
+4 to the resist roll** (per the Suicide Limit's softer-touch
application at R2).

## Steps

+1 additional instruction per Step (each requiring separate
rationalization).

## Special

After the instruction is carried out, the target remembers doing it but
may not understand why. They can rationalize it (*"I just felt like I
should put the gun down"*) or they can suspect manipulation (Insight
TN = your Channeling Sum to confirm suspicion).
