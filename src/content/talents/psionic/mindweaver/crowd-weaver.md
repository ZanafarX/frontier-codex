## Effect

Influence up to **20 non-Psionic targets** within Near range
simultaneously with a single emotional directive: *"Flee this place,"
"Ignore the strangers passing through," "Turn your anger toward the
Sheriff."*

The targets act on the emotion naturally — **this is mass Mood Shift,
not mass compulsion.**

## Resist

Only individuals with **ECHO 3+** may resist (ECHO + Focus vs. your
Channeling Sum). Everyone else is swept along.

## Steps

+10 additional targets per Step.

## Special

This is **riot-starting, mob-calming, revolution-igniting** power. It
affects civilians, crowds, and gatherings. **Military units with
discipline training** (Ironbrands, Consortium regulars) **gain +2 to
resist**. Psionics are immune entirely.
