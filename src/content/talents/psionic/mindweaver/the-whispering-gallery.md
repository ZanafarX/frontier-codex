## Effect

You open a telepathic network connecting all willing creatures within
Near range. For the rest of the scene:

- All connected creatures can communicate **telepathically** (no action
  required)
- All connected creatures share tactical awareness: **+2 dice** (Pool
  Bonus) to **Initiative, Awareness, and coordinated actions**
- You can relay sensory input between members (one person's eyes become
  everyone's)
- If one member spots an ambush or trap, **all members benefit from
  Danger Sense**

## Steps

+5m radius per Step.

## Requirement

**Cost to Others:** Each connected creature takes **1 Strain when the
scene ends**. The network is taxing.

## Special

**You are the hub.** If you are knocked unconscious or Disrupted, the
network collapses: all connected members take **1 Strain** from the
sudden psychic disconnect.

**Enemy Psionics can attempt to infiltrate the network** (ECHO + Channel
vs. your Channeling Sum to eavesdrop). The discipline namesake of
"The Whispering Gallery" is no accident: voices carry where you don't
want them to.
