## Effect

Target one creature within Near range. They become trapped in a loop of
their own surface thoughts. The target is aware of their surroundings
but cannot focus: they are **Dazed** (−3 dice Pool Penalty to all
actions) for **5 Ticks**.

They can still move, react, and defend, but complex actions
(**Channeling, Called Shots, Extended Checks**) are impossible.

## Resist

ECHO + Focus vs. your Channeling Sum ends the effect early (initial
roll on activation; further resist attempts at end of each affected
turn).

## Steps

+3 Ticks duration per Step.

## Special

Renamed from "Echo Chamber" in development to avoid conflict with the
Psy-Slinger Talent of that name. Both effects are distinct: Thought
Loop traps the target in their own mind; Echo Chamber (Psy-Slinger)
amplifies a sound to disrupt enemies.
