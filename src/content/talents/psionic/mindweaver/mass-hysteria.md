## Effect

All enemies within Near range are subject to the effect.

## Resist

ECHO + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum.

- **Failure:** Become **Frightened**. Flee from you for 1 round, then
  **Suppressed** for the scene.
- **Success:** No effect.

## Steps

+1 round of fleeing per Step.

## Special

If you score a **Critical (Margin 9+)**, the targets also **drop held
items** as they flee. Iron Calm (BS R1) can remove the Frightened
condition from any one ally afflicted.
