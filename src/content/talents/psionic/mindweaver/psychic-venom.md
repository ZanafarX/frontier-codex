## Effect

Plant a delayed psychic attack in the target's mind within Near range.
It activates when a condition you specify is met (examples: *"when they
fall asleep," "when they see a specific person," "when they draw a
weapon against me"*).

When triggered, the target takes **Strain equal to your Margin (max +5
from Margin)** and is **Stunned for 3 Ticks**.

**Duration:** The venom remains dormant for up to **24 hours**. If the
condition is not met, it dissipates harmlessly.

## Resist

ECHO + Focus vs. your **original Channeling Sum** (rolled at the time
of triggering, not planting).

## Steps

+1 Strain damage per Step (per the R3 ceiling: max 8 Strain combined
with Margin).

## Special

The target does not know the venom is planted. **Erasure Field (Bastion
Soul R3) or higher can detect and remove it**: Null Touch (R1) cannot
reach R3 effects per the Tier-Removal Hierarchy.
