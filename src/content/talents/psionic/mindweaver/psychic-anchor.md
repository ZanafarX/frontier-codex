## Effect

Touch a target. Mark them with a psychic tag. For **24 hours**, you
always know:

- Their general **direction and distance** from you
- Whether they are **alive or dead**
- Their current **emotional state**
- Whether they are **using Psionic abilities**

## Steps

+24 hours duration per Step.

## Special

Does not require line of sight after placement. The target feels nothing
(no awareness of the tag).

**Grounding (Bastion Soul R2) or higher removes the anchor** — Null
Touch (R1) cannot reach R2 effects per the Tier-Removal Hierarchy.
Useful for tracking fugitives, monitoring prisoners, or keeping tabs on
allies during split-party scenarios.
