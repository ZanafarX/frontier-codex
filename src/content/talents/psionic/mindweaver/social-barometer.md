## Effect

You gain an instant read of the **interpersonal dynamics** of all
creatures within Near range: who is the leader, who is lying, who is
afraid of whom, who wants to leave, who is attracted to whom, who has
secrets they're hiding from the others.

You gain **+2 Roll Bonus** to your next social Skill check in this
scene (Persuasion, Deceit, Insight, or Force), as you understand the
interpersonal landscape.

## Steps

+1 specific hidden detail per Step (secret alliances, who in the room
has betrayed someone here before, who is performing a role).

## Special

Does **not** detect threats or hostile intent; that is Crowd Pulse's
domain. Social Barometer reads social structure; Crowd Pulse reads
threat-context.
