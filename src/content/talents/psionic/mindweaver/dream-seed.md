## Effect

Touch a target. Implant a single idea, image, or emotion into their
**subconscious**. They will dream about it during their next Long Rest.
The dream feels meaningful and personal — the target is inclined to act
on it, **though they are not compelled**.

## Steps

+1 additional dream element per Step (more complex scenarios).

## Special

No Resist at the time of implantation. **During the dream**, the target
may make **ECHO + Insight (TN 13)** to realize the dream was planted.

This is the Mindweaver's primary tool for long-term manipulation. A
Consortium official who dreams about a specific location every night for
a week will eventually investigate. A guard who dreams about his family
being in danger will request a transfer. The effect is subtle,
deniable, and terrifyingly effective over time.
