## Effect

Alter minor details about your appearance in the perception of all
observers: change the color of your coat, the shape of your hat, a
scar's position, several distinguishing features.

The illusion is **visual only** and doesn't hold up to physical
inspection.

At your option, you may instead appear as a **specific person the
observer has met** (behavior differences may reveal the deception —
Insight vs. your Channeling Sum to notice).

**Duration:** Until you end it or take damage.

## Steps

+1 additional detail changed per Step.

## Special

Does **not affect mirrors, reflective surfaces, or photographs**. Your
true appearance shows in reflections. This is a narrative detail that
observant NPCs may notice.
