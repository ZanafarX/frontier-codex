## Effect

You adopt the **surface-level psychic signature** of another person
whose mind you have read with Surface Read. For the rest of the scene:

- **Insight checks** to identify you register the other person's
  emotional patterns
- **Surface Read on you** reveals the other person's surface thoughts
  (not yours)
- **Psionic detection abilities** identify you as the other person

## Steps

+1 hour duration per Step (beyond the scene).

## Special

This defeats **psionic security measures**: Consortium Psi-Division
checkpoints that scan for specific psychic signatures can be bypassed.
**Physical appearance is unchanged**; combine with Veil of Silence,
Liar's Mirror, or mundane disguise for full infiltration.
