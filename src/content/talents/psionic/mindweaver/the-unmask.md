## Effect

Target one creature within Near range. They are confronted with the
absolute truth about themselves — every self-deception, every
rationalization, every comfortable lie demolished in an instant.

## Resist

ECHO + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum.

| Failure Margin | Effect |
| :----: | :---- |
| Failure by 1–3 | **Stunned for 5 Ticks**; take **2 Strain**. Shaken but functional. |
| Failure by 4–6 | **Paralyzed for 10 Ticks**; take **4 Strain**. Existential crisis. |
| Failure by 7+ | Complete psychological collapse. **Downed (mentally) for the scene**. Take **1 Erosion**. |
| Success | **Suppressed for the scene** but otherwise resists. |

## Steps

+1 Strain damage per Step regardless of result.

## Special

**Bulwark modifiers:**

- Against targets with a strong self-image (**ECHO 4+, 5 intact
  Memories**), the Unmask is less effective: **+2 to their resist roll**.
- Against targets already in psychological distress (**Erosion 3+, 2 or
  fewer Memories**), it is devastating: **−2 to their resist roll**.
