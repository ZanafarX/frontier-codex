## Effect

Subtly alter a single target's emotional state within Near range: shift
calm to uneasy, irritation to fury, affection to suspicion. The change
**feels natural to the target**; they rationalize it as their own
reaction.

This does not compel action; it changes the emotional context in which
they make decisions.

## Resist

ECHO + Insight vs. your Channeling Sum to **notice the manipulation**
(not to resist the emotion itself). The emotion is imposed regardless;
the Resist roll only determines whether the target realizes what
happened.

## Steps

+1 additional target per Step.

## Special

Unlike The Nudge (which compels a specific action), Mood Shift adjusts
the emotional landscape. A guard shifted from "bored" to "anxious" is
more likely to investigate noises. A merchant shifted from "suspicious"
to "generous" might lower their prices. The GM determines behavioral
consequences.
