## Effect

Target one creature within Near range. They believe they are burning,
drowning, or being eaten alive.

The target takes **Strain damage equal to your Margin (max +5 from
Margin)**. The target is also **Stunned for 3 Ticks**.

## Resist

ECHO + Focus vs. your Channeling Sum negates the **Stun only**; the
Strain damage still applies.

## Steps

+1 Strain damage per Step (max **+3 from Steps**, for a hard ceiling of
**8 Strain at R3**).

## Special

**Armor provides no protection**: this is a mental attack. Iron Will
(BS R1) grants its standard +2 to resist the Stun (but not the Strain).
Absorb (BS R2) reduces the Strain by ECHO + IRON.
