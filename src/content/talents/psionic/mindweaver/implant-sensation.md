## Effect

You create a **false sensory experience** for one target within Near
range: a sound, smell, taste, or tactile sensation. They cannot
distinguish it from reality without a successful Insight check
(**TN = your Channeling Sum**).

**Duration:** 1 minute.

## Steps

+1 minute duration per Step.

## Special

**Examples:** The smell of smoke, the sound of approaching footsteps,
the feeling of bugs crawling, the taste of poison. The Insight check is
a recognition check (the sensation feels imposed once recognized), not
a Resist; the sensation occurs regardless.
