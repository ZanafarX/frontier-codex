## Effect

Target one creature within Near range. You fracture their psyche into
two competing personalities. For the rest of the scene:

- At the start of each of the target's turns, they must spend **1
  Mettle** or lose their action to internal conflict (the two halves
  argue, the body freezes, the hands work at cross purposes)
- If they **run out of Mettle**, they are **Stunned** until the effect
  ends
- All their actions suffer **−2 dice** (Pool Penalty) from the divided
  attention

## Resist

ECHO + Focus vs. your Channeling Sum negates. Can attempt to resist
again at the end of each turn at **−2 cumulative difficulty** (the
schism deepens with time).

## Steps

+1 Mettle drain per turn per Step.

## Special

If this brings a **Psionic target to 0 Mettle**, their connection to
the Current becomes unstable — any Channeling attempt triggers
**automatic Backlash** (roll as if one Bleed Die showed a 1).
