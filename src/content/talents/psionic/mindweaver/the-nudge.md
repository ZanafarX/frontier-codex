## Effect

Plant a simple, non-suicidal urge in the target's mind within Near range.

**Examples:** *"Look left," "Drop the coin," "Trust this stranger," "I
need a drink."*

The effect is instant. The target rationalizes the action afterward.

## Resist

ECHO + Focus vs. your Channeling Sum. On success, they hesitate but
don't obey.

## Steps

Target suffers −1 die (Pool Penalty) to resist per Step.

## Requirement

**Suicide Limit:** Cannot compel self-harm or obviously suicidal actions.
The Talent fails silently if the urge directly produces harm.
