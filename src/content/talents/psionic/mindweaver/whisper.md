## Effect

Send a short telepathic message (up to **10 words**) to a target. They
hear it in your voice, inside their head.

Range is line of sight, **or** anyone whose name and face you know
within **1 mile**.

## Steps

+1 additional target per Step.

## Special

Whisper is a **one-way message**. The target cannot respond unless you
have an active **Mind Link (R3)** with them. Bastion Soul Null Auras
suppress incoming Whispers within their radius.
