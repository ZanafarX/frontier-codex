## Effect

Target one creature within Near range. They suffer **−2 dice** (Pool
Penalty) to Awareness and Insight checks for the rest of the scene.
Thoughts slip sideways; observations blur.

## Resist

ECHO + Focus vs. your Channeling Sum negates.

## Steps

−1 additional die per Step (max **−4** total).

## Special

If used against another Psionic, they **also suffer −1 die to Channel
checks**. The static interferes with their connection to the Current.
