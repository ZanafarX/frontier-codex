## Effect

Create a complex visual and auditory illusion occupying up to a Close
range area. The illusion can include moving figures, sounds, smells,
and environmental details. It can depict anything you can imagine: a
campfire where none exists, a wall blocking a passage, a squad of
Consortium soldiers advancing.

**Duration:** Concentration (you must spend your action each turn to
maintain). Maximum **1 minute**.

## Steps

+1 minute maximum duration per Step.

## Requirement

**Interact:** Physical contact reveals the illusion: a hand passes
through. **Insight vs. your Channeling Sum** to see through it without
touching. **Animals and creatures with ECHO 4+ are not fooled.**

## Special

Concentration ends if you take damage and fail a Focus check (TN 11),
or if you Channel another Talent. The Grand Illusion is dramatic but
fragile; use it for misdirection, not as a sustained shield.
