## Effect

Touch a target. Implant a psychic prohibition in their mind. Choose a
specific category of thought or action: *"do not speak about what you
saw in the mine," "do not draw a weapon against me," "do not leave
this building."*

When the target attempts the prohibited action, they experience searing
psychic pain: **2 Strain** and they must make **ECHO + Resilience (TN
13)** to push through.

**Duration:** **1 week**. Can be renewed.

## Resist

ECHO + Focus vs. your Channeling Sum at the time of implantation. If
they succeed, they feel the prohibition being placed and know what was
attempted.

## Steps

+1 Strain on violation per Step.

## Special

**Reality Anchor (Bastion Soul R4) or higher can remove this** —
Grounding (R2) cannot reach R4 effects per the Tier-Removal Hierarchy.
Without a Bastion Soul, removal requires **another Mindweaver** or
**extreme willpower** (ECHO + Focus **TN 16**, once per day).
