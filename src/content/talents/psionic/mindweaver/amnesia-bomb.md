## Effect

All creatures within Near range (including allies, excluding yourself)
lose memory of the **last 5 minutes**. They retain their current
physical state (injuries, conditions, positions) but have no memory of
how they got there.

**You may selectively exclude allies from the effect at the time of
casting.**

## Resist

ECHO + Focus vs. your Channeling Sum negates (**each creature rolls
individually**).

## Steps

+5 minutes erased per Step.

## Special

**Physical evidence remains.** Spent shell casings, blood, bodies: all
present. The confusion is devastating in its own right. Guards don't
remember why they drew weapons. Witnesses don't remember the crime. The
erased minutes feel like waking from a blackout: deeply unsettling,
often causing panic.
