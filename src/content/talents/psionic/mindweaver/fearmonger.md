## Effect

Target one creature within Near range. They perceive their worst fear
manifesting.

## Resist

ECHO + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum. On failure, the target
becomes **Frightened** (flees from you for 1 round, then Suppressed
for the scene).

## Steps

+1 round of fleeing per Step.

## Special

Targets immune to Fear are immune to this Talent. Iron Calm (BS R1)
can remove the Frightened condition that Fearmonger imposes.
