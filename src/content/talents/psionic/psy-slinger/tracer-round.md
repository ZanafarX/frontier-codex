## Effect

Your next shot illuminates the target with spectral light. All attacks
against them gain a **−1 TN modifier** (easier to hit) until end of scene.
Multiple Tracers do not stack on the same target.

## Steps

−1 additional TN per Step (maximum −3 total cumulative).

## Special

Allies may benefit from the Tracer mark. Bastion Soul Null Touch removes it.
