## Effect

Your next shot ignores all physical defenses: **Armor Rating (AR)**,
**Cover**, and **Shields**. Damage is **WR + 3**. If the attack deals
damage to a target wearing Armor or using a physical Shield, that item is
**Sundered** (permanently loses 2 AR or is destroyed).

## Special

This is the universal physical-defense breaker. The Sunder effect is
permanent: armor and shields cannot be repaired without specialist
craftwork.

(No Steps: the rule-breaking + flat WR+3 is the talent's identity.)
