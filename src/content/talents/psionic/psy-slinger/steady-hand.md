## Effect

Remove the **Suppressed** condition from yourself. Gain a **+1 Roll Bonus**
to your next Ballistics check this scene.

## Steps

+1 Roll Bonus per Step (maximum +3).

## Special

Can be used as a **Reaction (Tempo 0)** by spending 1 Mettle in addition to
the Strain cost.
