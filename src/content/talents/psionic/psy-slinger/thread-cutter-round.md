## Effect

Your next shot, if it deals damage to a Psionic target, severs their
connection to the Current for the rest of the scene. The target cannot
Channel any Talents, cannot use Resonance, and loses any active psionic
effects they are maintaining. Their Erosion track does not reset: the
connection is *cut*, not *healed*.

## Resist

ECHO + Focus vs. your Channeling Sum. On success, the target retains their
connection but is still Suppressed for 5 Ticks.

## Steps

+1 hour beyond the scene per Step.

## Special

Against Veil-Born creatures that rely on the Current (Threadrippers,
Whispering Man), a successful Thread-Cutter forces them to make an
immediate Morale check at TN 15.
