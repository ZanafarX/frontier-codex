## Effect

You merge with your Bonded Focus firearm. For the rest of the scene, you
become a living weapon:

- Your body is partially metallic — gain **AR 3** (does not stack with
  worn armor)
- Your hand *is* the barrel: fire at **Tempo 2** (no draw required, ever),
  and your shots are exempt from Exposure
- Your attacks deal **WR + 2**
- You are immune to Disarm, and Salt Rounds deal only half their normal
  Disruption effect (Focus check allowed at +4 Roll Bonus)
- You can fire Ghost Bullets at will with no Strain cost (Rank 1 only —
  still requires a Channeling roll)

## The Price Beyond Memory

When the scene ends, the separation is agonizing. Take **1d8 Strain**
(excess converts to Erosion). Your weapon is undamaged, but your hand is
burned and blackened — −1 die to Finesse checks for 24 hours.

## Aftermath

You forget one sense permanently from the burned Memory. Not the Memory
itself (that's already gone) — but if the Memory was *"the smell of rain
on the day I fell in love,"* you will never fully smell rain again.
