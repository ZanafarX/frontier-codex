## Trigger

You may activate this at any moment, interrupting any other action.

## Effect

Time freezes. You may take **3 full Actions** immediately. During the
Waltz:

- No other character can move, react, or defend
- All targets' Defense drops to base 6
- All your shots deal maximum damage (treat as Margin 9+)
- You may move freely between shots

## Aftermath

When the Waltz ends, you fall Prone and take **1d8 Strain**.

## Special

If the Strain would exceed your Strain Cap, the excess becomes Erosion
instead.
