## Effect

Suppress a zone (Close range area) within your weapon's effective range.
All enemies who begin their turn in the zone or attempt to move through it
must make ECHO + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum or become Suppressed.
Lasts until your next turn. Consumes 3 rounds.

## Steps

+1 round of suppression per Step (extends to subsequent turns without
further action).

## Special

Does not deal damage. Area denial through psychic terror — the targets
*feel* the bullets tracking them. Uses standard Suppression rules.
