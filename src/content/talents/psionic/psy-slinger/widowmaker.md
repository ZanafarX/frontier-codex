## Effect

Your next shot, if it deals damage, plants a dormant charge in the target.
The charge detonates the next time the target takes a physical action
(attacking, moving more than a Shift, using an item). Detonation deals
damage equal to the original shot's damage again, ignoring AR. The target
must then make GRIT + Resilience (TN 13) or suffer a **Grievous Wound**.

## Steps

The target also takes +1 Strain per Step when the charge detonates.

## Special

The target *can feel* the charge. They know it's there. If they take no
physical actions, the charge fades harmlessly after 1 minute. This creates
a devastating tactical dilemma: act and suffer, or freeze and become a
non-threat.
