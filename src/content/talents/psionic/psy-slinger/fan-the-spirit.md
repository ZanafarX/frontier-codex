## Effect

Fire up to 6 shots at up to 6 different targets within Near range. Make
one Channeling roll; compare the Sum against each target's Defense
individually. Consumes 6 rounds (the full cylinder).

**Damage:** Standard WR per hit; AR applies separately per target.

## Steps

+1 damage to all hits per Step.

## Restriction

**Each shot must target a different enemy.** If fewer than 6 enemies are
in range, excess shots miss harmlessly.

## Special

If you have fewer than 6 bullets in the cylinder, fire what you have.
