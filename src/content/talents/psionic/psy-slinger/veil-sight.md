## Effect

For the next minute, you can see through solid objects (up to **1 meter**
thick), through magical darkness, and through illusions. Hidden creatures
and invisible beings become visible to you.

## Steps

+1 minute duration per Step.

## Special

Wall thickness penetration is fixed at 1m. Veil-Sight does not penetrate
Bastion Soul Null Auras.
