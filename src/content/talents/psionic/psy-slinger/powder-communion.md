## Effect

Touch a firearm. You learn the following about the last time it was fired:

- Who held it (a psychic impression — face, emotional state, hand size)
- What it was aimed at (a flash of the target)
- Whether the shot killed

## Steps

+1 additional firing event per Step (further back in time).

## Special

If the weapon was used in a murder, you also learn the general direction
to the body (if it hasn't been moved). Works on any firearm, not just your
own.
