## Effect

For the next minute, you gain **+2 Roll Bonus** to Awareness checks to
detect hidden or concealed creatures within your weapon's effective range.
Your gun subtly pulls toward threats — a faint tug in the wrist.

## Steps

+1 minute duration per Step.

## Special

This does not grant Veil-Sight or the ability to see through walls. It
tells you *where* to look, not *what* is there. Useful for detecting
ambushes and hidden snipers.
