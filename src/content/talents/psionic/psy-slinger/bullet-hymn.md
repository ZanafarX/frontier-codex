## Effect

Craft a single bullet from a burned Memory. This bullet exists as a
physical object and can be stored indefinitely. When fired (by anyone,
from any firearm), the Bullet Hymn:

- Automatically hits any target the firer names aloud
- Deals **10 damage**, ignoring AR and all defensive effects
- The target must make ECHO + Resilience (TN 16) or lose **1 Memory** of
  their own (only Psionic characters and NPCs with Memory slots are
  affected; mundane targets take **3 Erosion** instead)

## The Relic

The bullet can be given to allies, sold, or hidden. It is a physical
object — about the size of a normal round but cold to the touch and
faintly luminescent. Anyone who holds it hears a distant hum.

## Special

Only one Bullet Hymn can exist at a time. Creating a second destroys the
first. The bullet remembers what it was made from — the firer experiences
a flash of the burned Memory at the moment of firing.
