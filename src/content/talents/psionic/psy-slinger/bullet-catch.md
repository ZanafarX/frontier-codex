## Trigger

You are hit by a ranged attack using a physical projectile (bullet, arrow,
thrown weapon).

## Effect

Reduce incoming damage by your **ECHO** rating. If the damage is reduced
to 0, you catch the projectile. A caught bullet can be loaded into your
weapon as a free action — it is now infused with the Current and deals
**+1 WR** on its next firing.

## Steps

+1 damage reduction per Step.

## Special

Does not work against Psionic attacks, explosions, or non-physical
projectiles (Ghost Bullets, psychic bolts).
