## Effect

Target one visible enemy holding a firearm within Near range. Their weapon
jams; it cannot be fired until they spend an action clearing it
(**Tempo 4** for the target).

## Resist

GRIT + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum negates.

## Steps

+1 additional weapon per Step (different targets).

## Special

Does not work on weapons that aren't firearms. Has no effect on melee
weapons, bows, or psionic focuses.
