## Effect

Fire 3 shots at a single target in rapid succession. Make one Channeling
roll. If it exceeds the target's Defense, all 3 shots hit. Total damage =
**WR × 3, calculated as a single damage instance (AR applies only once).**
Consumes 3 rounds.

## Steps

+1 Roll Bonus per Step (improves hit chance, not extra shots).

## Special

Fusillade is the only Psy-Slinger talent that calculates AR-once on
multi-hit. This is its defining feature: against high-AR targets
(Hardsuits, Bosses), the burst damage punches through where individual
shots would not.
