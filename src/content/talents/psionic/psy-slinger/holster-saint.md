## Effect

For the rest of the scene, your Bonded Focus firearm cannot be disarmed,
stolen, or knocked from your grip. If separated from you, it returns to
your hand at the start of your next turn (no action required, any range up
to Far). Additionally, while Holster Saint is active, your **Draw Weapon**
action (normally Tempo 2) is reduced to **Tempo 0**: you can draw and act
in the same Tick.

## Steps

+1 additional weapon protected per Step (must touch each to activate).

## Requirement

Must be used with a Bonded Focus.

## Special

Combined with Quick-Draw Hex, Holster Saint enables a Psy-Slinger to swap
and fire weapons without losing Tempo. The weapon's loyalty becomes a
tactical engine.
