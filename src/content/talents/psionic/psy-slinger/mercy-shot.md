## Effect

Fire a shot at a willing ally. The bullet phases through their flesh
without dealing damage. Instead, it removes one of the following
conditions: **Suppressed**, **Blinded** (temporary), or **Stunned**. The
target also clears 1 Strain.

## Special

Must hit the ally (roll against their Defense, but they can choose not to
Dodge/Dive). If you miss, nothing happens; the bullet is still physical
if it strikes an unintended target.
