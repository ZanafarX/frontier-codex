## Effect

For 1 minute (10 Ticks), all weapons within Far range become inert.
Firearms will not fire. Blades will not cut. Fists connect but deal no
damage. Psionic attacks that deal direct damage fail automatically. Only
environmental hazards and the elements remain lethal. Consumes 1 round.

## Duration

1 minute. Nothing can end this effect early except another Miracle.

## The Silence

During The Armistice, the world goes quiet. No gunshots, no screams of
violence. The Tension Pool is **emptied entirely**. When the minute ends,
reality reasserts itself violently: add **2 dice** to the Tension Pool.

## Special

This affects allies as well as enemies. Both sides are disarmed. What
happens during that minute (negotiation, flight, confession, betrayal)
is up to the people in it.
