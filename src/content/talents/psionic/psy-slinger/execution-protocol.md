## Effect

Make a single Ballistics attack with **+4 Roll Bonus**. If the target is
Downed, Paralyzed, Stunned, or Grappled, the attack automatically hits and
deals maximum damage (treat as Margin 9+).

## Steps

+1 WR per Step.

## Special

Against healthy, mobile targets, this functions as a powerful but slow
aimed shot with the +4 bonus. The auto-hit only applies to incapacitated
targets.
