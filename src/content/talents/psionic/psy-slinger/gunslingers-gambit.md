## Effect

If this is your last bullet, the attack gains: **+4 Roll Bonus**, **+3 WR**,
and the **Breaker** quality. If the attack results in a Critical (double
8s), the target also suffers a **Grievous Wound** (roll on the table) in
addition to all other damage.

## Requirement

Your weapon must have exactly 1 round remaining. If you have more, this
Talent cannot be activated.

## Steps

+1 WR per Step.

## Special

This talent embodies the Frontier's love of desperate wagers. If you miss,
you're empty and Exposed.
