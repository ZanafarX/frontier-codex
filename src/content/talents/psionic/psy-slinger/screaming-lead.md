## Effect

Your next shot deals normal damage and forces the target to make an
ECHO + Resilience check vs. your Channeling Sum. Failure: the target gains
**1 Erosion**. This is one of the only ways to inflict Erosion on another
creature through combat.

## Steps

+1 Strain to the target per Step (regardless of Erosion result).

## Special

Veil-Born creatures are immune to the Erosion effect (they are already of
the Current). All other creatures, including mundane humans, have an
Erosion track (ECHO + 5) and are vulnerable. Against a mundane target,
this can be devastating — an untrained mind has little Erosion capacity to
spare.
