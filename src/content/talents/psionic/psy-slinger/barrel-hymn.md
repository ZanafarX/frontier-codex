## Effect

Touch a firearm (yours or an ally's). For the rest of the scene, the
weapon is treated as one Quality tier higher (Poor → Standard, Standard →
Superior, Superior → Masterwork). Masterwork weapons gain no additional
benefit.

## Steps

+1 additional weapon per Step (must touch each).

## Special

The weapon faintly hums at a pitch only the wielder can hear. Psionics and
Veil-Born within Close range can detect this as Channeling.
