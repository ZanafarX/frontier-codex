## Effect

Fire a single shot at any target you have ever seen, regardless of
distance, obstacles, or line of sight. The bullet exits reality, travels
through the Veil, and re-enters at its destination. The attack
**automatically hits**. Damage = **WR × 3**, ignoring AR, Cover, and all
defensive psionic effects. Consumes 1 round.

## The Cost

After the shot, your Bonded Focus crumbles to rust. It cannot be repaired.
You must bond a new weapon (a process that takes 1 week of dedicated use
and a successful ECHO + Channel TN 13 check).

## Aftermath

You take 1d6 Strain and are Blinded for 1 minute. Your eyes show you where
the bullet went — through the Veil, through the screaming dark, through
the space between.
