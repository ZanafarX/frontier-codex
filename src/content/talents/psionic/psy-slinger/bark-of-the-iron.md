## Effect

Your next shot functions as an IRON + Force (Intimidation) check in
addition to an attack. Use your Channeling result as the Intimidation roll
against all enemies within Near range who witness the shot. Targets who
fail their resist are **Suppressed until the end of your next turn**.

## Steps

−1 additional die (Pool Penalty) to affected targets per Step.

## Special

The shot does not need to hit its target to trigger the Intimidation
effect — a deliberate miss works. If you intentionally miss, the shot
deals no damage but the Intimidation still applies.
