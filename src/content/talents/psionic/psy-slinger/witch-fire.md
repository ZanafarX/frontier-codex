## Effect

Your next shot deals normal weapon damage. If it hits, the target takes
**1 Strain** immediately.

## Steps

Per Step, the target takes an additional 1 Strain delivered 5 Ticks after
the original hit (max +3 Strain delivered, at 5 / 10 / 15 Ticks after the
shot).

## Special

Strain is delivered regardless of distance. The target experiences it as
creeping psychic frostbite.
