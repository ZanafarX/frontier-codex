## Effect

Whether the attack hits or misses (Manifest + Hit OR Manifest + Miss), the
impact creates a sonic boom. The target and all enemies within
**Close Range** of the impact point must make a **GRIT Check (TN 11)**.
Failure: they are **Suppressed** (cannot take Attack actions on their
next turn).

## Steps

Targets who fail are also **Deafened** (−2 Awareness) for **5 Ticks per
Step**.

## Special

The sonic boom affects all creatures in the radius, including allies. The
shot itself still rolls vs. the primary target's Defense for damage.
