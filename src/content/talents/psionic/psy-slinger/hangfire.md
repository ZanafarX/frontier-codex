## Effect

Fire a shot that embeds in a surface (wall, floor, tree, person's armor).
The bullet remains inert until you speak a command word (free action, any
range). On command, the bullet detonates: **WR 2** damage in a Close range
radius. Against a target the bullet is embedded in, the damage ignores AR.
Consumes 1 round (the embedded bullet is no longer in your magazine).

## Steps

+1 active hangfire bullet per Step (each detonates independently on its
own command).

## Duration

The bullet remains dormant until triggered or until your next Long Rest.

## Special

Maximum 1 active hangfire at a time without Steps invested.
