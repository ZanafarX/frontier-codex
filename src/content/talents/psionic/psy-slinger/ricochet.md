## Effect

Your shot bounces off a hard surface to hit a target in Hard Cover or
around a corner. The attack ignores Cover penalties to TN.

## Steps

+1 bounce per Step (each subsequent bounce can hit an additional target,
make a separate Channeling Sum vs. Defense check for each new target, or
navigate complex geometry).

## Requirement

Must have a hard surface to bounce from (stone, metal).

## Special

Bounces cannot circle back to a target already hit.
