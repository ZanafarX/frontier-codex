## Effect

Declared as a free action before an attack. If you are currently
**Wounded** (at or below half Vitality), your next shot gains **+1 WR**
base.

## Steps

+1 WR per Step (maximum +2 from Steps).

## Special

If you are **Downed** and can still act (crawling, speaking), the base
bonus becomes **+2 WR**. Steps still apply normally (max +2). The Frontier
rewards stubbornness.
