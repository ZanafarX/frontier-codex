## Effect

Your next shot becomes **Ethereal**. It passes through non-magical barriers
up to **30cm (1 ft)** thick to strike a target on the other side. You must
suspect a target is there but do not need Line of Sight. The shot deals
full damage to **Ethereal entities** (Spirits, Wraiths) which normally
resist physical damage.

## Steps

+30cm (1 ft) penetration per Step.

## Special

Damage to non-Ethereal targets is standard WR.
