## Effect

Fire a shot at a location where a moving target *will be* when the bullet
arrives. This attack **cannot be Dodged**. The target cannot spend Mettle
on Dive or Dodge reactions. The attack otherwise resolves normally (roll
vs. Defense).

## Steps

+1 Roll Bonus per Step.

## Special

Requires that the target has moved at least once in the last 5 Ticks.
Stationary targets can't be predicted.
