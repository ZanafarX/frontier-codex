## Effect

Your next shot gains **+2 WR** and the **Knockdown** quality. If the
target is Knocked Prone, they are also pushed back **3 meters**.

## Steps

+1 WR per Step (maximum +1 from Steps).

## Special

Destroys Light Cover entirely on hit. Push distance is fixed at 3m.
