## Effect

Your next shot, if it deals damage, leaves a glowing psychic brand on the
target visible only to you. For the rest of the scene, you always know the
target's exact location (even through walls), their general health state
(Healthy/Wounded/Downed), and whether they are moving or stationary.

## Steps

+1 hour duration per Step (beyond the scene).

## Special

The brand fades at dawn. A Bastion Soul's Null Touch removes it instantly.
