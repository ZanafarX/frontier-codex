## Effect

For **1 hour**, you can track any creature that is currently bleeding
(including Wounded creatures, those with Bleed conditions, or anyone who
has shed blood in the last hour) by scent alone.

You gain **+4 Roll Bonus** to Frontier (Tracking) checks against
bleeding targets.

## Steps

+1 hour duration per Step.

## Special

This works in conditions where visual tracking fails — darkness, rain,
ash storms. The scent is vivid to you: you can distinguish between
different individuals' blood and estimate how much has been lost.
