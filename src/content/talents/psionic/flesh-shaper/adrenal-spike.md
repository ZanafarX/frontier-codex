## Effect

Touch a target. They gain **+2 IRON** and **+2 QUICK** (Attribute
bonuses, not dice) for the rest of the scene. These increase relevant
pools, Vitality, Defense, etc.

The target also becomes **immune to Fear** and **ignores Wound penalties**.

## Steps

+1 to either IRON or QUICK per Step (choose each time).

## Requirement

**Attribute Cap:** Maximum **5** in either Attribute from this Talent
(excess is lost). This keeps Adrenal Spike below the R4 Apex Predator
and R5 Apotheosis ceilings.

## Special

**The Crash:** When the scene ends, the target takes **1d3 Strain** and
is **Exhausted** (−2 dice Pool Penalty to all physical actions until
they rest).

Removable by Grounding (BS R2) or higher per the Tier-Removal Hierarchy.
