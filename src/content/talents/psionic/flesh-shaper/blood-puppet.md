## Effect

Target one creature within Near range. You seize control of their
circulatory system. For **10 Ticks**:

- You control their blood pressure, heart rate, and oxygen distribution
- You can **Immobilize** them (reduce blood flow to extremities — limbs
  go numb)
- You can cause **Blindness** (restrict blood flow to eyes) or
  **Deafness** (restrict to ears)
- You can cause **unconsciousness** (stop blood flow to brain — target
  falls Downed but is not injured; they wake when you release them or
  when someone shakes them vigorously)
- You can **kill them** (stop the heart — target takes **4 damage per
  Tick**, ignoring AR, until the heart resumes)

## Resist

GRIT + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum, **each turn**, to break free.

## Steps

+5 Ticks duration per Step.

## Special

This does **not affect creatures without blood** (constructs, spirits,
some Veil-Born). Against living targets, it is one of the most
terrifying abilities in the game — there is no defense except raw
endurance and hope. The Suicide Limit does NOT apply (this controls the
body, not the will).
