## Effect

Mark a target with a powerful pheromone signature. For **24 hours**:

- All animals within Far range can detect the target and are drawn
  toward or repelled from them (caster's choice)
- The target **cannot benefit from Stealth** against creatures with
  scent-based detection
- Other Flesh Shapers automatically detect the mark (it's a professional
  calling card)

## Resist

GRIT + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum negates.

## Steps

+24 hours duration per Step.

## Special

In the **Fen**, this is a death sentence — Fen-Wraiths hunt by spiritual
scent, and the pheromone mark makes the target a beacon. In the
**Scorchveil**, it attracts Sand Mites and worse. Context determines
severity.
