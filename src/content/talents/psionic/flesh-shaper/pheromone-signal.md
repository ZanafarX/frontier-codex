## Effect

Alter your scent profile for **1 hour**. Choose one:

- **Calming:** Animals within Near range will not attack you unless
  provoked. **+2 Roll Bonus** to Handling checks for the rest of the scene.
- **Threatening:** Predators and hostile animals within Near range must
  make a Morale check (**TN 9**) or withdraw. Horses may bolt (Ride check
  **TN 11** for mounted enemies).
- **Neutral:** You have no scent. Tracking dogs and scent-based hunters
  cannot follow you. Skin-Changers cannot detect you by smell.

## Steps

+1 hour duration per Step.

## Special

People consciously notice nothing, but their subconscious responds —
discomfort around a Threatening Flesh Shaper, ease around a Calming one.
Useful in social situations the caster can't control with words.
