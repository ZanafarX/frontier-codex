## Effect

Infect the target with an accelerated disease (touch; melee attack roll
if unwilling).

## Resist

GRIT + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum.

- **Failure:** The target contracts **Wasting Sickness**. They take **1
  damage per hour** (ignoring AR) and suffer **−1 die** (Pool Penalty)
  to all physical actions until cured. The disease runs its course in
  **24 hours** if untreated.
- **Success:** The target takes **2 Strain** and feels violently ill for
  10 minutes but suffers no lasting effect.

## Steps

+1 damage per hour per Step.

## Special

**Cure:** Medicine check (**TN 15**), **Purge** (from another Flesh
Shaper — including the caster), or **24 hours of misery**.

The ethical weight of creating disease is enormous. The Circle of Ash
teaches Plague Touch as a deterrent and last resort. Flesh Shapers who
use it casually are shunned — even by other Psionics. **For contagious
plague, see Pandemic (R4).**
