## Effect

Target one creature within Near range. One of their physical Attributes
(IRON, GRIT, QUICK, or SAVVY — **not SWAY or ECHO**, which are mental)
is temporarily reduced by **1**. This affects all derived statistics
(Vitality, Defense, Speed, pools). Lasts for the rest of the scene.

## Resist

GRIT + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum negates.

## Steps

−1 additional Attribute point per Step (can target the same or different
Attribute, maximum **−3** total).

## Special

The withering is visible — muscles sag, skin grays, the target moves as
if aged. When the effect ends, the Attribute returns and the target
feels a rush of returning vitality. **Repeated targeting of the same
creature in a single day extends the duration by 1 hour per additional
use.**
