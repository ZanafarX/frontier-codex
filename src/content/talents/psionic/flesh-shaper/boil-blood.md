## Effect

Target one creature within Near range.

## Resist

GRIT + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum.

- **Failure:** Takes **4 damage** (ignores armor) and is **Blinded** as
  capillaries burst in their eyes.
- **Success:** Takes **2 damage**.

## Steps

+1 damage per Step (**maximum +2 from Steps**; cap **6 damage** on
failure to stay within R3 ceiling).

## Special

Creatures **without blood** (constructs, some Veil-Born) are **immune**.
Iron Will (BS R1) does NOT defend against Boil Blood — this is
biological, not mental.
