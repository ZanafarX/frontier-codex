## Effect

Touch a willing target. Perform permanent biological modification.
Choose **one**:

- **Enhanced Senses:** +1 die (Pool Bonus) to Awareness permanently.
  Eyes glow faintly.
- **Reinforced Skeleton:** +1 Vitality permanently. Bones are denser —
  you sink faster in water.
- **Accelerated Reflexes:** +1 to Initiative permanently. Hands twitch
  at rest.
- **Enlarged Organs:** +1 to Strain Cap permanently. Abdomen is slightly
  distended.
- **Thickened Skin:** +1 AR permanently (does NOT stack with worn armor).
  Skin is leathery and discolored.

## Steps

Can perform +1 modification in a single session per Step (each counts
toward the maximum).

## Requirement

A character can receive a **maximum of 3 Biosculpts total**. Each one
after the first inflicts **1 permanent Erosion** (the body is drifting
from human baseline).

Combined with Adaptive Evolution (R4), the hard cap is **5 maximum
permanent modifications** per character.

## Special

Biosculpted individuals are visibly altered. Consortium Psi-Division can
identify them on inspection. Redeemers consider them abominations. The
modifications cannot be reversed except by another Flesh Shaper of equal
or greater skill. **Fresh Biosculpts** can be erased by Erasure Field
(BS R3) or higher; **older, integrated** Biosculpts are part of the body
and cannot be removed by anti-Psionic Talents.
