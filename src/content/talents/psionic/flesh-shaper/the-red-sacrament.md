## Effect

**Trigger:** A creature within Touch range has just died (within 1 round
of death).

You convert the dying creature's life force into healing energy. All
willing creatures within Near range heal HP equal to the **dead
creature's maximum Vitality**. Additionally:

- All Grievous Wounds on affected creatures are **cleared**
- All Strain on affected creatures is **cleared**
- All Bleeding conditions stop
- All temporary conditions (Suppressed, Blinded, Stunned) end

## Requirement

The creature must have been alive within the last round; you cannot
use this on a corpse that's been dead for hours. The dead creature's
body is **consumed**: reduced to ash and dust. There is nothing left
to bury.

This is **not a Reaction.** The trigger establishes when the Talent
can be cast, but the Channeling takes the full Tempo 5 (during which
the caster can be interrupted, attacked, or Disrupted normally).

## Special

**The Cost Beyond Memory:** You burn a Memory and gain **2 Erosion**
immediately (the act of converting death to life tears at your soul);
this is reflected in the compound cost.

The ethical dimension is brutal: to save your allies, you must watch
someone die and harvest them. **Some Flesh Shapers have killed wounded
enemies specifically to fuel this Talent.** The Circle does not discuss
it openly.
