## Effect

Touch the target (melee attack roll if unwilling). You reshape their
skeleton into a prison. Their ribs extend and interlock, their spine
curves, their limbs fold inward. The target is **Immobilized and cannot
take physical actions** for **1 minute**. They can still speak, see,
and use purely mental abilities.

## Resist

IRON + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum.

## Steps

+1 minute duration per Step.

## Requirement

If the target has **AR 2+**, the armor prevents the reshaping (you
can't reach the bones through plate).

## Special

Releasing the target requires the caster's touch (Tempo 3) or a
**Medicine check (TN 15)** by someone else. Forced release without
medical expertise deals **1d3 damage** (bones snap back violently).
