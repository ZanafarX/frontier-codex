## Effect

Immediately stop a **Bleeding** condition on a target (including
Arterial Hit from the Grievous Wound table). The wound is sealed —
messily, painfully, but completely. The target takes **1 Strain** from
the pain but is no longer losing HP per turn.

## Steps

+1 Strain recovered on the target per Step (the cauterization also burns
away exhaustion).

## Special

Unlike Knit Flesh (which heals HP and stabilizes), Cauterize is faster
(Tempo 3 vs. 5) and specifically targets Bleeding conditions **without
healing damage**. It's triage, not treatment.
