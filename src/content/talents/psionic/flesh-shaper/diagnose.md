## Effect

Touch a creature. You learn:

- Current HP and any Grievous Wounds
- Any diseases, poisons, or parasites
- Any Psionic effects currently affecting them
- General health (age, chronic conditions, recent injuries)

## Steps

+1 piece of hidden information per Step (secret injuries, pregnancies,
old wounds that never healed right).

## Special

Iron Will (BS R1) does not block Diagnose; this reads the body, not
the mind. The target may sense the touch as warm pressure, but cannot
prevent the read short of refusing contact.
