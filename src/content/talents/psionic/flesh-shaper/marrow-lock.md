## Effect

Target one creature within Near range. They must roll Resist (see below)
or be **Immobilized** (Speed 0) for **5 Ticks**. Their bones lock in
place — they can still speak, breathe, and move their eyes, but their
limbs, spine, and jaw are rigid.

## Resist

IRON + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum.

## Steps

+3 Ticks duration per Step.

## Special

This is not Paralytic Touch (which requires physical contact and targets
muscles). Marrow Lock works at range and targets the **skeleton
directly**. Creatures without bones (oozes, spirits, some Veil-Born) are
**immune**.
