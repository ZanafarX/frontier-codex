## Effect

You must touch the target (requires melee attack roll if unwilling).
They must roll Resist (see below) or be **Paralyzed** (cannot take
physical actions) for **5 Ticks**.

## Resist

GRIT + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum.

## Steps

+3 Ticks duration per Step.

## Special

Target can still speak, see, and use purely mental abilities — the
paralysis is muscular, not neurological. This distinguishes Paralytic
Touch from Marrow Lock (which targets bones) and Bone Cage (which
encages the skeleton).
