## Effect

Touch a creature. Remove one of the following:

- **Poison** (ends effect immediately)
- **Disease** (ends effect; doesn't heal damage already done)
- **Bleed** condition
- One **Rank 1–2 Psionic effect** (any discipline)

## Steps

+1 additional effect removed per Step.

## Special

Purge is the same-discipline cure for Plague Touch (FS R3). It does NOT
cure Pandemic at scale: Purge ends an individual infection, but cannot
stop transmission across a population (that requires Reality Anchor or
killing the original source).
