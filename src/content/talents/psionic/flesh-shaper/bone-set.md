## Effect

Treat a **Flesh Wound** or **Broken Bone** result from the Grievous
Wound table **without requiring a Medicine check**. The wound is healed
instantly through psionic manipulation — bones realign, torn ligaments
fuse, dislocations snap back.

## Steps

Can treat a **Concussion** per Step (more delicate — reshaping bruised
brain tissue).

## Special

The treatment is **agonizing**. The target takes **1 Strain** from pain.
There is no anesthetic in the Red Weave — you feel everything being
moved inside you. Targets who have never experienced a Bone Set before
must make **ECHO + Resilience (TN 9)** or become **Suppressed for 3
Ticks** from shock. The Talent works regardless of the shock check.
