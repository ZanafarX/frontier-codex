## Effect

Touch a willing target or fresh corpse. Create a functioning biological
organ, graft, or prosthetic from available tissue. You can:

- Replace a **lost eye, finger, or ear** (functionality restored, but
  the replacement looks slightly wrong — different color, different
  texture)
- Create a biological **splint** that heals a Broken Bone permanently in
  1 hour instead of 1 day
- **Graft skin** over a burn or acid wound, removing the cosmetic damage
  and any associated penalties
- Grow a **functional replacement limb** (requires 1 hour of work and a
  corpse to harvest tissue from — the limb works but is visibly stitched)

## Steps

+1 organ complexity tier per Step (Step 1 = replacement hand; Step 2 =
functional eye; Step 3 = replacement lung or internal organ).

## Special

Replacement parts from corpse tissue always look *slightly wrong*. The
skin tone doesn't match, the fingernails grow too fast, the eye is the
wrong color. This is the Flesh Shaper's signature — their work is
functional but unmistakably artificial in its biology.
