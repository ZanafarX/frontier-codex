## Effect

Repair an item made of **organic material** (leather, wood, rope, bone,
sinew) by stimulating its cellular memory. The item returns to **Standard
Quality** regardless of its current state — torn leather reseals, snapped
rope re-braids, cracked wood knits.

## Steps

Can repair +1 additional item per Step.

## Special

Does **not** work on metal, glass, stone, or synthetic materials. A
Flesh Shaper can repair a wooden stock but not a gun barrel, mend a
saddle but not horseshoes, fix a rope bridge but not iron chains.
