## Effect

Touch the initial target. You create a virulent psionic plague that
spreads on contact. The initial target is infected (as Plague Touch, but
more severe: **2 damage per hour**, **−2 dice** Pool Penalty).

At the end of each hour, every creature that has been within Close range
of an infected individual must make **GRIT + Resilience (TN 13)** or
become infected themselves.

**Duration:** The plague runs for **48 hours** or until cured.

## Resist

Initial target: GRIT + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum (per Plague
Touch). Transmission: GRIT + Resilience (TN 13) per hour of exposure.

## Steps

+1 damage per hour per Step.

## Requirement

**Cure:** Purge ends an individual infection. Destroying the **original
target** before they spread it stops the plague at its source. **A
Bastion Soul's Reality Anchor (R4) or higher stops transmission within
its radius.** Null Zone (BS R3) suppresses within its area but does NOT
stop transmission outside the zone.

## Special

This is a **weapon of mass destruction**. Use it against a Consortium
garrison, and the garrison falls without a shot fired. Use it near
Palomera, and you may kill the only sanctuary the Hollowborn have. The
Circle of Ash **formally forbids** this Talent. Flesh Shapers who use
it are exiled or killed by their own.

**The Tension Pool gains +2 dice when Pandemic is activated**: the
Current loves suffering.
