## Effect

Touch a creature. They ignore **Wound penalties** (the −1 die Pool
Penalty for being below half HP) for the rest of the scene.

## Steps

+1 additional target per Step (must touch each).

## Special

Does **not heal damage** — only masks the pain. If the target reaches 0
HP, they collapse immediately with **no warning**. The body has been
lying to them about how badly it's hurt.
