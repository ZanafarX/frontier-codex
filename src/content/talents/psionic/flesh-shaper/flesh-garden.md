## Effect

Touch the target (melee attack roll if unwilling). You implant a
biological construct inside them — a mass of living tissue that grows,
feeds on the host, and obeys your commands. The construct deals **1
damage per turn** (ignoring AR) and can be commanded to:

- **Restrain:** The host is **Immobilized** as internal tendrils grip
  their skeleton
- **Silence:** The host cannot speak as the construct presses on vocal
  cords
- **Torment:** The host takes **2 Strain per turn** instead of 1 damage
  (psychic pain from the growth)
- **Erupt:** The construct tears free, dealing **4 damage** to the host
  and creating a small, short-lived biological servitor (HP 4, Pool 4,
  WR 2, lasts 1 minute)

**Duration:** Until the construct is removed (Purge, Medicine TN 15
surgery) or until it Erupts.

## Resist

GRIT + Resilience vs. your Channeling Sum prevents implantation.

## Steps

+1 damage per turn per Step.

## Special

This is **body horror incarnate**. The host can feel the construct
moving inside them. It is, by any reasonable standard, torture. The
ethical implications should give players pause.
