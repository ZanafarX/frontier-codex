## Effect

Introduce a mild toxin or stimulant into a consumable substance or
directly into a wound. Choose one:

- **Sedative:** Target becomes drowsy. **−2 dice** (Pool Penalty) to
  Awareness for 1 hour. Extended exposure (3+ doses) causes deep sleep.
- **Stimulant:** Target recovers **1 Strain**. If used more than twice
  in 24 hours, subsequent doses cause 1 Strain instead (the body
  crashes).
- **Tracer:** The target's breath and sweat gain a faint metallic tang
  detectable only by you. You can track them by scent within Near range
  for **24 hours**.

## Steps

+1 dose produced per Step.

## Special

The substance is undetectable to normal senses. **Medicine (TN 13)** or
Diagnose identifies it. Purge removes it.
