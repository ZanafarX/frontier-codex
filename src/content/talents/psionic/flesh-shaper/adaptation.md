## Effect

Touch a creature. Grant them resistance to **one** environmental hazard
for **1 hour**. Choose:

- **Heat / Cold:** Ignore Strain from extreme temperature exposure
- **Toxin:** **+4 Roll Bonus** to resist poison and disease; immune to
  Ash Belt exposure
- **Pressure:** Can function underwater or in thin air without penalty
- **Darkness:** Gain limited darkvision (can see shapes and movement in
  complete darkness, but not fine detail)

## Steps

+1 additional resistance per Step (granted to the same target).

## Special

The adaptation is **visible**: skin thickens, pupils dilate enormously,
veins darken. This marks the target as psionically altered, which may
cause social problems.
