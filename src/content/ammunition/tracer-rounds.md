Tracer rounds glow as they travel, marking their path through the air and
illuminating their target on impact. They're used to coordinate fire,
adjust aim, and signal allies in the dark.

**Effect:** +1 follow-up bonus. Any ally firing at the same target on
their next action gains +1 to their Final Sum.
