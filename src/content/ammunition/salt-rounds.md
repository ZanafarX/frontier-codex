Salt rounds are the standard anti-supernatural ammunition. The salt
disrupts the Current, making these rounds devastating against spirits,
Hollow Men, and other Veil-born creatures. Against living targets,
they're painful but less lethal than lead.

**Effect:** WR −1 vs. living targets; WR ×2 vs. spirits and Veil-born;
+1 Roll Bonus vs. supernatural targets; on hit against a Psionic, causes
automatic Disruption (no save).
