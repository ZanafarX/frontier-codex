Ghost-rock rounds are powerful, yet terrifyingly unstable.
The ghost-rock fragments detonate on impact, dealing massive damage. But
the volatility works both ways; a fumble can be catastrophic for the
shooter.

**Effect:** +2 WR on hit; weapon firing these rounds gains the Volatile
quality for the duration of the magazine.
