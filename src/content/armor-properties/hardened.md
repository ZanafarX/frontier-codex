---
name: "Hardened"
short_effect: "Negates the first Grievous Wound of the scene (damage still applies)."
tags: ["mitigation"]
---

Hardened armor is built to absorb a single catastrophic impact before
buckling: overlapping plates, reinforced cores, sacrificial layers. The
damage from the Grievous Wound still happens; the wound itself does not.
Once the protection is spent, the armor reverts to its normal AR for the
remainder of the scene.
