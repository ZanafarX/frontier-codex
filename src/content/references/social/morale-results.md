---
name: "Morale Results"
category: "social"
description: "What happens after a Morale check, based on success or degree of failure."
columns:
  - key: "result"
    label: "Result"
  - key: "behavior"
    label: "Behavior"
rows:
  - result: "Success"
    behavior: "Stands firm. Continues fighting."
  - result: "Failure by 1–3 (Shaken)"
    behavior: "−2 dice to all actions until rallied or threat removed."
  - result: "Failure by 4–6 (Breaks)"
    behavior: "Attempts to flee. Will surrender if cornered."
  - result: "Failure by 7+ (Routs)"
    behavior: "Panicked flight. Drops weapons, screams, tramples allies."
notes:
  - "Rallying: a leader can spend Tempo 4 to roll SWAY + Force or Persuasion vs. TN 11 to rally a Shaken or Broken ally. Success: normal morale. Critical: target gains +1 die to all actions for rest of scene."
  - "Routed NPCs cannot be rallied in combat. They are gone."
  - "Surrendered enemies become NPCs under GM control with appropriate behavior (negotiating, trying to escape if pressed, betraying captors at opportunity)."
---

Combat that ends in surrender is more memorable than combat that ends
in extinction. A Routed bandit who comes back two sessions later with
a vendetta is better drama than a corpse.
