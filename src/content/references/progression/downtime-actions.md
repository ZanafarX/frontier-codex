---
name: "Downtime Actions"
category: "progression"
description: "Activities available during Downtime between sessions. Each character gets one Downtime Action per day of downtime."
columns:
  - key: "action"
    label: "Action"
  - key: "effect"
    label: "Effect"
rows:
  - action: "Recover"
    effect: "Long Rest: Heal IRON HP, clear all Strain, attempt to clear 1 Grievous Wound (Medicine check)."
  - action: "Therapy"
    effect: "Clear 1 Erosion via Vice, or 2 Erosion via Anchoring (requires scene with Anchor-related NPC)."
  - action: "Acquire"
    effect: "Shop for items (Scrip) or Scrounge for rare items (SAVVY + Scrounge vs. TN 13)."
  - action: "Train"
    effect: "Spend XP to raise Attributes, Skills, or learn Talents. Talents require a source (Mentor / Grimoire / appropriate Skill)."
  - action: "Work"
    effect: "Earn 1d10 Scrip through labor, gambling, or odd jobs."
  - action: "Forge Scar"
    effect: "Fill an empty Memory slot (8 XP, requires roleplay scene). See the Memory Palace rules."
  - action: "Research"
    effect: "SAVVY + Lore to learn about a location, enemy, artifact, or mystery."
  - action: "Craft"
    effect: "Create ammunition, simple tools, or modify equipment (requires materials)."
notes:
  - "Typical downtime length: 1-3 days between sessions in town; 1 week for major story break; 1+ month for time skips (handled as montage)."
  - "Therapy via Vice: GM may require roleplay and impose consequences — lost money, hangovers, reputation damage, addiction risk after 3 consecutive uses of the same Vice."
  - "Therapy via Anchoring is the healthiest method: a meaningful scene with someone connected to your Anchor Memory clears 2 Erosion."
---

Downtime is where Psionics survive. Without a Vice or an Anchor, the
Erosion track only fills. The party that doesn't take downtime is the
party that goes Hollow.
