Resilience covers resisting poison, disease, exhaustion, environmental
hazards, and psychological pressure. It's the skill of simply refusing to go
down. Resilience pairs primarily with GRIT, but pairs with IRON for
torture-style endurance and with ECHO for resistance to supernatural fear.
