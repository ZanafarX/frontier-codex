Young zealots carrying torches and pouches of rock salt. Most are
between fifteen and twenty, recruited from frontier orphanages and
refugee camps by Redeemer preachers who offered food, safety, and
answers. They believe with the absolute certainty of the young and
the traumatized. They will die for the cause because the cause is the
first thing that ever made them feel like they mattered.

This is what makes them dangerous and what makes killing them haunt
you.

Gear: Torch, 3 salt pouches, holy symbol (wooden), threadbare robes,
letter from home.

## Tactics

Mob tactics. Surround suspected witches in groups of 3–5. Throw salt
to disrupt Channeling, then close with torches. They fight with more
courage than skill, and they scream prayers while they do it.

## Morale

Fanatical (−4) when facing "witches." Normal when facing mundane
threats; they signed up to fight the supernatural, not bandits.
