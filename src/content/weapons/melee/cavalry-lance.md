The cavalry lance is built for one purpose: the first strike of a charge.
Its length keeps the wielder safely beyond a defender's blade, and the
horse's momentum drives the point home.

**Charge bonus:** +1 WR (effective WR 5) on the first attack of a mounted
charge against a target the lance reaches at full speed.
