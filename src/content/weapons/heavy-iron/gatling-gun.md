The Gatling is the most devastating infantry weapon on the Frontier. Its
multiple rotating barrels allow continuous fire as long as ammunition feeds
and the crank turns.

**Mounting required:** The Gatling cannot be fired from the hip. It must
be mounted on a tripod, wagon, or fortification.

**Crew:** Optimal use requires two crew — a gunner to aim and crank, and a
loader to feed the belt. Solo operation suffers −2 dice and rate-of-fire
limitations.

**Ammunition:** Belt-fed; capacity is effectively limited only by belts on
hand.
