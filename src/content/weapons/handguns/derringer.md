The derringer is a gambler's gun, a lady's gun, a last-resort gun. It holds
only two shots and has poor range, but it fits in a boot, a sleeve, or a
corset. Many a man has died surprised by a derringer he never saw coming.

**Restriction:** Cannot fire effectively at Far or Extreme range.
