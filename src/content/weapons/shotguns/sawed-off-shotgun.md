The sawed-off sacrifices what little range a shotgun has for portability and
sheer terror. It's the weapon of highwaymen, enforcers, and anyone who
expects to fight in spaces measured in feet rather than yards.

**Range restriction:** Effective only at Point-Blank. Close range attacks
suffer +4 TN. Cannot engage at Near or beyond.
