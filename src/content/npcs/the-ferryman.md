A mute giant who poles a flatboat through the swamp. Knows safe paths
through the **Thousand-Lantern Fen** (the field of corpse-candles
that lures travelers to drowning) and the seasonal currents of the
Two-Mouth Spring.

The Ferryman is never named. His price is whatever feels right when
you climb aboard; he accepts it silently and adjusts his expression
not at all. Pay too little and you simply don't get the trip you
needed. Pay too much and you've established a precedent that the rest
of the Fen will hear about.

## Tactics

The Ferryman is a Soldier-tier combatant who would rather not fight.
His real weapons are the boat (which he steers with terrifying skill
through hazards no one else navigates) and the pole (which is
weighted iron and very long). He has never been seen to draw a knife
or a gun.

## Plot Anchor

Any plot that requires reaching a specific point in the Fen (the
swamp center for Mother Elaine's herb, a Brood Mother island, the
Cypress Cathedral) requires the Ferryman. He chooses whether the
trip happens, and his refusal cannot be bought once given.
