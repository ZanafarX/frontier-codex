The Winch-Master controls the only route between the Bottom and
the High-Rock. If he doesn't like you, you don't go up.

This makes him, functionally, the most powerful person in Greystone
above the level of Mayor Krell. Krell commands; the Winch-Master
decides who Krell ever gets to command.

## Notes for the GM

The Winch-Master has no published stat block because his power is
**logistical, not martial.** He's an Ironbrand-trained engineer who
runs the lift's steam systems, the filter protocols at the cliff
base, and the records of who has and hasn't yet completed the
three-day Ashborn-screening wait.

In a combat encounter he is a Soldier-tier civilian — competent with
a wrench, unwilling to fight if the lift isn't already broken. His
real threat is **denial of access.** Anyone who needs into Greystone
on their schedule, not his, has a problem only he can solve.

## Plot Anchor

In the "Sabotage" hook, the Winch-Master is either the saboteur or
the man best positioned to identify the saboteur. The GM decides
which.
