Unlicensed Flesh Shaper in a butcher shop back room. Doc Holliday
reattaches limbs for §50. He keeps the off-cuts in jars on a shelf
behind the cleavers. He claims they're for "research." Most of the
research seems to involve cataloging which patients survive a year
without the original tissue.

He operates out of the back of Hennessey's Butchery on The Strip,
which has the advantage of legitimate hooks and saws. The arrangement
is mutually beneficial. Hennessey doesn't ask. Holliday doesn't
explain.

## Tactics

Holliday is not a fighter but he is a Flesh Shaper, and a desperate
one can do unsettling things to a body in close quarters. He prefers
to avoid combat entirely; if cornered, he uses Knit Flesh on himself
or close allies and tries to talk his way out. He surrenders quickly
when threatened by anyone competent.

## Plot Anchor

If a PC needs grievous-wound treatment in Rustwater and can't afford
the legitimate doctor (or the legitimate doctor is bought), Holliday
is the answer. The price is always §50, and there is always a price
beyond §50 that he won't name until you're on the table.
