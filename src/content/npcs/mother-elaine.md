The matriarch of Bellhaven. A powerful Flesh Shaper who treats
**Fen-Rot** — the slow biological decay that afflicts everyone who
breathes the Fen's air long enough.

Mother Elaine charges in service, not Scrip. Debts to her
accumulate, and she remembers every one. The most dangerous
sentence in Bellhaven is, "Mother Elaine has called for you." The
second most dangerous is, "I'll see you when you owe me less than
you do now."

## Tactics

Mother Elaine does not initiate combat. She is older than her HP
suggests and physically frail, but a Flesh Shaper of her tier with
seven Channel dice and ECHO 4 can change the parameters of a fight
in ways most enemies do not survive. Her preferred response to
threats is to know about them well in advance, through the network of
Bellhaven residents who owe her.

## Plot Anchor

The "Cure" plot hook (herb needed from the swamp center, guarded by
a Fen-Wraith) is hers. The price of the cure she'd give to a PC is
the cure itself, and her debts are the currency of every long
Bellhaven campaign.
