Head of the Consortium's Psi-Division: the unit that registers,
manages, and exploits Psionics under the Orvain Protection Contracts.
Cold, efficient Mindweaver. Wears gloves at all times to hide the
Veil Bleed scars climbing her forearms.

Kael is the rarest kind of Mindweaver: one who has read every other
Mindweaver's mind in her city and never met one as careful as
herself. She doesn't enjoy the Psi-Division's work. She just believes
the alternative (unmanaged Psionics, witch-burnings, civil
collapse) is worse.

The Circle of Ash has tried to recruit her three times. Each
recruiter has disappeared. She has not retaliated openly; she has
simply ensured the conversation never repeats.

## Tactics

Kael leads from the office, not the field. When she does engage
directly (usually for a defection, a Circle infiltration, or a
particularly volatile unregistered caster), she goes in personally
because she does not trust her subordinates with the kind of decisions
she might need to make. Her preferred opener is a Rank 3 Mindweaver
Talent that lets her *know* whether the target is willing to be
brought in alive.

## Tactical Notes

Kael has no canonical attack stat block in source. The Boss-tier
Pool 9 represents her Channel pool for Mindweaver Talents. She wears
a Consortium Vest (AR 2) under her professional dress. Treat her
Talents as the published Mindweaver Discipline content. She
specializes in the social and intel layers (Surface Read, The Nudge,
Phantom Pain) rather than the bigger Powers.
