This is the site of the **First Rupture** — the original tear where
the Veil was first opened, and the wound from which everything else
has bled. The Devil's Eye has never closed. It never will.

Travelers use the Ojo for navigation because it is the only feature
large enough to see through the Scorchveil's heat-haze. Most travelers
who reach its shore do not return. Those who do return wrong.

## Environmental Effects

| Effect | Mechanic |
| :----- | :------- |
| Dead Zone | Animals refuse to approach within 1 mile. Mounts must be tethered and blindfolded. |
| Cold | Temperature drops to freezing within 100m of the edge, even at noon. |
| Time Dilation | 1 hour at the shore = 1 day in the outside world. **ECHO TN 13** to notice time passing. |
| Tension Amplifier | The Tension Pool gains **+1 die** for every hour spent at the Eye. |

## Legends

Locals throw offerings into the water — coins, guns, fingers — hoping
the Devil looks away. Retrieving an offering is said to grant a wish.
The price is always ironic tragedy.

## Mechanical Use

- Powerful artifacts may be found at the edge (GM discretion).
- **Entering the water is death.** No save. The traveler is dissolved
  into the Current; their soul rejoins the original tear.
- Seers gain **+2 dice** to prophecy-related checks near the Eye, but
  take **1 Erosion** per use. The Eye sees clearly. It also sees back.

## GM Notes

Ojo del Diablo is the Frontier's nuclear option for plot stakes — a
place where the answer to "what is the Veil?" is closest to retrievable.
It should be treated as the most dangerous fixed location on the map.
A Circle of Ash expedition that returns from the Eye usually returns
short members, and the survivors are different.

The Fading Ward plot hook for Palomera (replacement Ley-Crystal must
be retrieved from the Eye) is one of the canonical ways to bring PCs
here. Most other paths in are paid in blood.
