Mindweavers are the most reviled of the Disciplines. They deal in illusions,
compulsions, and the violation of the mind's sovereignty. No wall can stop
them, no armor can protect from them, and worst of all, you might never
know they touched you at all.

## Attack-Replacing Talents: Universal Rule (Resist-Based Variant)

The same Manifest / Manifest+Miss / Fizzle framework that governs
Psy-Slinger attack-replacing Talents applies to Mindweaver Talents that
target a creature. The difference is the variant: most Mindweaver Talents
**do not "attack" in the conventional sense**: they offer a **Resist** roll
instead of forcing a Defense roll. When a Resist is offered:

1. **Manifest:** Channeling Sum ≥ TN. The power forms in the Current.
2. **Resist:** The target rolls their Resist pool against the **same
   Channeling Sum**. Success = the Talent has no effect on them. Failure =
   full effect applies.

If a Talent has **no Resist clause**, it cannot be opposed; the effect
simply happens on a successful Manifest. Examples: Whisper (R1), Liar's
Mirror (R1), Veil of Silence (R2), Mind Link (R3), The Whispering Gallery
(R4), Soul Merge (R5).

### Common Resist Pools

| Pool | Used When | Example Talents |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| ECHO + Focus | Most compulsion, mental intrusion, and identity attacks | The Nudge, Babel, Suggestion, Confessional, Implant Memory, Puppet Strings |
| ECHO + Resilience | Fear, trauma, and forced experience effects | Fearmonger, Shriek, Ego Death, The Unmask, Mass Hysteria |
| ECHO + Insight | Effects you might *notice* but not strictly resist | Mood Shift, False Friend, Liar's Mirror disguise checks |

The Iron Will trait (Bastion Soul R1) and the Mental Defense Bonus from the
Bulwark (intact Memory count) both feed into these rolls per their standard
rules.

## No Reaction Convention

Unlike Bastion Souls and Seers, Mindweavers have **no Reaction Convention**.
The discipline has exactly **one** Reaction Talent (Psychic Flinch, R1), and
it pays the full standard cost, **1 Mettle + 1 Strain**, every time.
There is no "first free per scene" discount. Mindweavers are not reactive
defenders; they are slow, premeditated infiltrators. The Flinch is a
deliberate outlier.

## The Suicide Limit

This is the discipline's **hard ethical brake**: codified in the mechanics,
not just the fiction:

> **No Mindweaver compulsion or mind-control Talent can force a target to
> directly harm themselves.** If a Talent's controlled action would cause
> the target to shoot themselves, walk off a cliff, drink poison, or
> otherwise commit self-harm with clear intent and awareness, the control
> **breaks immediately** and the target takes **1d6 Strain** instead (per
> Puppet Strings' formulation).

### Affected Talents

- **Puppet Strings (R4):** explicit Suicide Limit in the talent text
- **The Chorus (R5):** explicit Suicide Limit; applies per-individual
  (the Limit breaks one creature's control, not the entire mass effect)
- **The Nudge (R1):** codified as "Cannot compel self-harm or obviously
  suicidal actions" in the Limit field
- **Suggestion (R2):** codified via "+4 to resist" against direct
  self-harm orders; the Limit doesn't auto-break, but resistance is
  effectively decisive at that bonus

### Important Carve-Outs

The Suicide Limit is **mind-control specific**. It does NOT apply to:

- **The Wet Strings (Flesh Shaper R3):** pulls muscles, not will. The
  target can be forced to harm themselves.
- **Blood Puppet (Flesh Shaper R4):** controls the circulatory system, not
  the mind. Can stop the heart directly.
- **Indirect consequences of compulsion:** a target ordered to "walk
  outside" who walks into traffic still gets hit; the Mindweaver didn't
  order them to be hit, just to walk.

This is why Flesh Shapers are feared as much as Mindweavers, arguably
more, because Iron Will and Poker Face don't help against a finger on the
carotid artery.

## Key Mechanical Notes

- **Strain damage caps.** R3 direct-damage Talents (Phantom Pain, Psychic
  Venom) cap Margin contribution at **+5** to prevent runaway scaling.
  Steps add up to +3 on top, for **max 8 Strain damage at R3**.
- **The Bulwark interaction.** Targets with all 5 Memories intact gain
  **+2 to resist identity-targeting Talents** (Ego Death, The Unmask,
  Memory Palace Siege). This is a deliberate mechanical reward for
  Memory preservation.
- **Psionic immunity to mass control.** Psionic characters are immune to
  The Chorus. Their connection to the Current shields them from
  mass-control effects. Individual control Talents (Puppet Strings,
  Mind Fortress) still affect Psionics normally.
- **Bastion Soul Tier-Removal anchors.** Persistent Mindweaver Talents
  explicitly call out which Bastion Soul Rank can remove them:
  - **Psychic Anchor (R2)** → Grounding (BS R2) or higher
  - **Psychic Venom (R3)** → Erasure Field (BS R3) or higher
  - **Implant Memory (R3)** → Grounding (BS R2) or higher *(R2 reaches
    R2 effects, but Implant Memory is also vulnerable to confrontation
    with contradictory evidence, the Mindweaver-specific reversal)*
  - **Mind Fortress (R4)** → Reality Anchor (BS R4) or higher
- **The Bond ceiling.** R5 Talents that involve psychic invasion (The
  Rewrite, Soul Merge, The Hollow Crown, The Silence) can only be
  reversed by another Miracle. These are intended as permanent
  narrative consequences, not encounter-scope debuffs.
