Flesh Shapers manipulate living tissue: knitting wounds, accelerating
healing, and reshaping flesh into new configurations. Their power is
visceral, intimate, and deeply unsettling to witness. The sounds alone,
wet tearing, grinding bone, the pop of resetting joints, are enough to
turn strong stomachs.

## No-Reactions Principle: Structural Identity

Flesh Shapers are **the proactive discipline.** Across all 50 Talents,
**none are Tempo 0 (Reaction).** This is intentional; flesh sculpting
requires time, intent, and deliberate concentration. A Flesh Shaper cannot
dodge a bullet, but they can heal it after. They commit and live with the
consequences.

This creates a meaningful asymmetry with Seer (the reactive discipline)
and a complementary partnership: a party with both has perfect preparation
AND perfect response.

### The Red Sacrament Clarification

The Red Sacrament (R5) has a **trigger condition** ("a creature within
Touch range has just died within 1 round") but is **Tempo 5**, not Tempo 0.
It does not violate the No-Reactions principle. It's a triggered action
with full Channeling time, not a free Reaction; the Flesh Shaper still
spends 5 Ticks performing the conversion, during which they can be
interrupted, attacked, or Disrupted normally.

## Cross-Discipline Interactions: Catalog

These references honor the Bastion Soul Tier-Removal Hierarchy. Each Flesh
Shaper effect is removed only by Bastion Soul Talents of equal or higher
Rank. This catalog is the canonical reference for how Bastion Souls
counter Flesh Shaper output.

### Active Effects (Removable While Active)

| Flesh Shaper Effect | Rank | Removable By |
| :---- | :---: | :---- |
| Adrenal Spike, Iron Skin | R2 | Grounding (BS R2) or higher |
| Biosculpt *(fresh)* | R3 | Erasure Field (BS R3) or higher |
| Adaptive Evolution *(active)* | R4 | Reality Anchor (BS R4) or higher |
| Marrow Forge *(active)* | R4 | Reality Anchor (BS R4) or higher |
| Apex Predator, Chimera Form *(active)* | R4 | Reality Anchor (BS R4) or higher; The Citadel (BS R5) can end early |
| Apotheosis *(active)* | R5 | Reality Anchor (BS R4) or The Citadel (BS R5) |

### Permanent Modifications (Not Removable by Anti-Psionic Talents)

Older Biosculpts and any healed Adaptive Evolutions become **part of the
body**. Bastion Soul Talents cannot strip them away; only another Flesh
Shaper of equal or greater skill can reverse the modification. This
distinction is important: a fresh Biosculpt on an enemy can be erased; a
year-old one is permanent biology.

### Area / Network Effects

- **Pandemic (R4):** Reality Anchor (BS R4) or higher stops transmission
  within its radius. Null Zone (BS R3) can suppress within its area but
  does not stop transmission **outside** the zone.
- **Hivemind Pulse (R4):** Bastion Souls within range create "dead zones"
  in the biological web (sensory only, no Talent required, just their
  presence).

### Long-Form Constructs

- **Symbiote (R5):** Killable only by **The Citadel (BS R5)** or
  **Reality Anchor (BS R4)** sustained over multiple Ticks. Lower-Rank
  Bastion Soul Talents cannot destroy a Symbiote.

### Same-Discipline Cures

- **Plague Touch (R3):** Curable by **Purge (FS R2)** within the same
  discipline. Pandemic, however, propagates beyond Purge's per-target
  scope; the only true counters are Reality Anchor or destroying the
  initial source.

### Iron Will Does Not Defend Against Biological Reads

The Bastion Soul's mental fortress doesn't help against a finger on the
carotid artery. **Iron Will (BS R1)** does NOT defend against:

- Vital Read (FS R1: reads heart rate, micro-expressions)
- Empathic Anatomy (FS R1: reads physical sensation)
- Boil Blood (FS R3: targets the circulatory system directly)
- Any Flesh Shaper effect that targets the body rather than the mind

This is one of the most important inter-discipline notes in the game. A
party that thinks Iron Will is universal psychic armor learns otherwise
the first time a Flesh Shaper diagnoses their captain's gout.

## Key Mechanical Notes

- **No Reactions.** Flesh Shapers commit to actions and live with
  consequences. They are the proactive discipline. (The Red Sacrament is
  not an exception; see above.)
- **Attribute caps.** Adrenal Spike (R2) and Apex Predator (R4) both cap
  at Attribute 5 max from the Talent. This keeps both below R5 Apotheosis
  (Attribute 6). Consistent with Bastion Soul's Titan Form ceiling.
- **Damage caps.** Boil Blood (R3) capped at +2 from Steps (max 6 damage
  on failure) to stay within R3 ceiling. Life Leech (R4) capped at 9
  damage at full Steps: R4 single-target ceiling.
- **The Suicide Limit applies to mind-control (Mindweaver) but NOT to
  body-control (Flesh Shaper).** The Wet Strings (R3) and Blood Puppet
  (R4) can force the target to harm themselves. The Flesh Shaper isn't
  influencing will; they're pulling muscles.
- **Permanent modifications stack with hard caps.** 3 Biosculpts + 2
  Adaptive Evolutions = **5 maximum permanent modifications** per
  character. Each beyond the first inflicts permanent Erosion.
- **Mass Mend's Strain conversion can kill the caster** if they
  overcommit to healing. Strain that exceeds the cap converts to HP
  damage. A Flesh Shaper who Mass Mends a full party of injured allies
  may drop themselves to 0 HP. This is the discipline's most dangerous
  self-cost mechanic.
- **Caster's Tax (Knit Flesh, Regeneration, Blood Rouse, Mass Mend).**
  These healing Talents charge the caster Strain, sometimes per HP
  healed, sometimes per turn. The Flesh Shaper literally bears the
  burden. Body prose explains each Talent's specific Tax; frontmatter
  cost shows only the Channeling cost.
