A satchel charge is a Rail-Jack's heavy demolition tool. Devastating, but
unstable: the volatile quality applies to handling as well as
detonation.

**Damage:** WR 6, Area Attack (5m radius). Carries the Volatile quality;
on a Fumble while handling, the charge detonates immediately.
