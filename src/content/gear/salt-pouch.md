Salt is the Frontier's primary anti-supernatural defense. A salt pouch
contains enough for ten applications: drawing a salt circle around a
camp, throwing a handful at a Psionic to disrupt their channeling, or
warding a doorway against spirits.

**Use:** Disrupting a channeling Psionic causes automatic Disruption with
no save. A salt circle around a camp prevents Tension Pool buildup
overnight.
