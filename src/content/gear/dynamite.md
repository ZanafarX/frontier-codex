Dynamite is a Muck-Raker's everyday tool and a Rail-Jack's favored
solution. A single stick can clear a tunnel, breach a wall, or end a
fight. The fuse burns at 5 ticks per second of length; standard cuts
detonate ~3 Ticks after lighting.

**Damage:** WR 4, Area Attack (3m radius). Treat as a thrown weapon
attack using QUICK + Ballistics.
