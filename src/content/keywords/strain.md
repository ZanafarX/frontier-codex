Strain is the short-term cost: the body keeping score of every shot
of Current you've channeled, every hour you've spent in toxic air,
every Fear check you've muscled through. Unlike Erosion, Strain is
**temporary**: it heals with rest.

| GRIT | Strain Cap |
| :---: | :---: |
| 1 | 4 |
| 2 | 5 |
| 3 | 6 |
| 4 | 7 |
| 5 | 8 |

Strain accumulates in stages, with progressively worse mechanical
penalties:

| Stage | Strain Level | Effect |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Winded** | 1 to ½ Cap | None (symptoms only) |
| **Fatigued** | ½ Cap to Cap−1 | −1 Initiative die; no Mettle refresh |
| **Depleted** | At Cap | −1 die to all rolls; no Mettle refresh; no Bleed/Desperation |
| **Overwhelmed** | Over Cap | Excess converts to Vitality damage |

Recovery:

| Method | Strain Cleared |
| :---- | :---- |
| Short Rest (10 minutes) | 1 (adds +1 Tension Die; cannot if Tension ≥ 4) |
| Laudanum (consumable, addictive) | 1d3 |
| Long Rest (8 hours, safe location) | All |
| Purge (Flesh Shaper R2) | 1 |
| Medical attention (1 hour of care) | All |

The Overwhelmed conversion is one of the game's hardest pressure
points: a Psionic at full Strain who casts a Rank 2 Talent takes 2 HP
of direct damage on top of the failed conservation. Power *always*
finds a way to extract its tax.
