The Veil is the wall that should keep our world separate from the
**Current**: the chaotic dimension beyond. Before the **Rupture**, the
Veil was whole, and the Current only seeped through in trickles that
the old shamans called dreams or madness. After the Rupture, the Veil
is **a rotting fabric** that vibrates with every gunshot, burns with
every spell, and tears further every time someone Channels.

**Visual metaphors used throughout the rulebook:**

- "Water pressing against a cracking dam" (the Current behind the Veil)
- "A wound that will not close" (the Rupture)
- "Threads worn to lace" (the Fen, where the boundary is thinnest)
- "Reality is rotting fabric" (general state of the Veil post-Rupture)

**Where the Veil is thinnest:**

| Location | Severity |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Ojo del Diablo** | Severe (Stable Breach, the First Rupture site) |
| **The Fen of the Fallen** | Moderate-Severe (dead walk freely) |
| **The Uphill River** | Moderate (when water flows) |
| **Bell-in-the-Black** | Moderate (centered on the monolith) |
| **Red Mother's Teeth** | Mild (blood-mineral deposits) |
| **Red Mother's Maw** | Total tear (the Maw IS a Thin Place given form) |

The **Tension Pool** is the in-table representation of the Veil's
local stress: every die added is another tug at the threads. A Pool
Rupture is the Veil failing in real-time at that location.

**Bastion Souls** are the discipline dedicated to anchoring and
sealing the Veil. Their Rank 5 Miracle, **The Seal**, permanently
closes a Breach at the cost of 1 ECHO and a Memory: the most
expensive single act in the game, and the discipline's reason for
existing.
