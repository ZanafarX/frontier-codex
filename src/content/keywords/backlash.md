Backlash is the immediate consequence of pushing past your limits. When
you add **Bleed Dice** to a roll (or trigger **Cursed Dice** through
Desperation), each 1 that appears on those special dice contributes to
the Backlash count. Only **Bleed and Cursed Dice** can cause Backlash:
1s on your regular pool are simply low rolls.

| Bleed 1s | Effect | Severity |
| :---: | :---- | :---: |
| One 1 | **The Flicker:** Take 1 Strain. The GM gains +1 die to the Tension Pool. Reality shudders: lights flicker, temperatures shift. | Minor |
| Two 1s | **The Tear:** Take 1 Erosion. The Current has reached deeper than you intended. | Moderate |
| Three 1s | **The Breach:** Burn 1 Memory immediately. The Talent succeeds with maximum effect (treat Margin as 12+ regardless), but you'll never remember why. | Severe |

The probability is real but manageable: with 3 Bleed Dice, the chance
of at least one 1 is **~33%**, two 1s is **~4.4%**, and three 1s is
under **0.2%**.

**Fumble Backlash** is a separate but related effect: when a Channeling
roll Fumbles (Margin −6 or worse), the GM describes a discipline-flavored
catastrophe (Psy-Slinger bullet curves wild, Mindweaver illusion turns
real, etc.) in addition to the standard Fumble effects.

Every Backlash also adds to the **Tension Pool**: The Current notices
the tear you've made and presses against the wound.
